维摩诘所说经

尔时释提桓因于大众中白佛言。世尊。我虽从佛及文殊师利闻百千经。未曾闻此不可思议自在神通决定实相经典。如我解佛所说义趣。若有众生闻是经法。信解受持读诵之者。必得是法不疑。何况如说修行。斯人即为闭众恶趣开诸善门。常为诸佛之所护念。降伏外学摧灭魔怨。修治菩提安处道场。履践如来所行之迹。世尊。若有受持读诵如说修行者。我当与诸眷属供养给事。所在聚落城邑山林旷野有是经处。我亦与诸眷属。听受法故共到其所。其未信者当令生信。其已信者当为作护。佛言。善哉善哉。天帝。如汝所说。吾助尔喜。此经广说过去未来现在诸佛不可思议阿耨多罗三藐三菩提。是故天帝。若善男子善女人。受持读诵供养是经者即为供养去来今佛。天帝。正使三千大千世界如来满中。譬如甘蔗竹苇稻麻丛林。若有善男子善女人。或一劫或减一劫。恭敬尊重赞叹供养奉诸所安。至诸佛灭后。以一一全身舍利起七宝塔。纵广一四天下高至梵天表刹庄严。以一切华香璎珞幢幡伎乐微妙第一。若一劫若减一劫而供养之。于天帝意云何。其人植福宁为多不。释提桓因言。多矣世尊。彼之福德若以百千亿劫说不能尽。佛告天帝。当知是善男子善女人。闻是不可思议解脱经典信解受持读诵修行福多于彼。所以者何。诸佛菩提皆从是生。菩提之相不可限量。以是因缘福不可量佛告天帝。过去无量阿僧祇劫时。世有佛号曰药王如来应供正遍知明行足善逝世间解无上士调御丈夫天人师佛世尊。世界名大庄严。劫曰庄严。佛寿二十小劫。其声闻僧三十六亿那由他。菩萨僧有十二亿。天帝。是时有转轮圣王名曰宝盖。七宝具足主四天下。王有千子。端正勇健能伏怨敌。尔时宝盖与其眷属供养药王如来。施诸所安至满五劫。过五劫已告其千子。汝等亦当如我以深心供养于佛。于是千子受父王命。供养药王如来。复满五劫一切施安。其王一子名曰月盖。独坐思惟。宁有供养殊过此者。以佛神力空中有天曰。善男子。法之供养胜诸供养。即问。何谓法之供养。天曰。汝可往问药王如来。当广为汝说法之供养。即时月盖王子行诣药王如来稽首佛足。却住一面白佛言。世尊。诸供养中法供养胜。云何为法供养。佛言。善男子。法供养者。诸佛所说深经。一切世间难信难受。微妙难见清净无染。非但分别思惟之所能得。菩萨法藏所摄。陀罗尼印印之。至不退转成就六度。善分别义顺菩提法。众经之上入大慈悲。离众魔事及诸邪见。顺因缘法。无我无人无众生无寿命。空无相无作无起。能令众生坐于道场而转法轮。诸天龙神干闼婆等所共叹誉。能令众生入佛法藏摄诸贤圣一切智慧。说众菩萨所行之道。依于诸法实相之义。明宣无常苦空无我寂灭之法。能救一切毁禁众生。诸魔外道及贪著者能使怖畏。诸佛贤圣所共称叹。背生死苦示涅槃乐。十方三世诸佛所说。若闻如是等经。信解受持读诵。以方便力为诸众生分别解说显示分明。守护法故。是名法之供养。又于诸法如说修行。随顺十二因缘。离诸邪见得无生忍。决定无我无有众生。而于因缘果报。无违无诤离诸我所。依于义不依语。依于智不依识。依了义经不依不了义经。依于法不依人。随顺法相无所入无所归。无明毕竟灭故。诸行亦毕竟灭。乃至生毕竟灭故。老死亦毕竟灭。作如是观。十二因缘无有尽相。不复起见。是名最上法之供养佛告天帝。王子月盖从药王佛。闻如是法得柔顺忍。即解宝衣严身之具。以供养佛白佛言。世尊。如来灭后我当行法供养守护正法。愿以威神加哀建立。令我得降魔怨修菩萨行。佛知其深心所念。而记之曰。汝于末后守护法城。天帝。时王子月盖见法清净。闻佛授记以信出家。修集善法精进不久。得五神通逮菩萨道。得陀罗尼无断辩才。于佛灭后以其所得神通总持辩才之力。满十小劫。药王如来所转法轮随而分布。月盖比丘以守护法勤行精进。即于此身化百万亿人。于阿耨多罗三藐三菩提立不退转。十四那由他人深发声闻辟支佛心。无量众生得生天上。天帝。时王宝盖岂异人乎。今现得佛号宝炎如来。其王千子即贤劫中千佛是也。从迦罗鸠孙陀为始得佛。最后如来号曰楼至。月盖比丘即我身是。如是天帝。当知此要。以法供养于诸供养为上。为最第一无比。是故天帝。当以法之供养供养于佛

逐句解释

天帝问佛：何谓法供养——供养的最高形式

天帝释（帝释天）问佛：在一切供养中，何者最胜？佛陀答：法供养（以法作供养）是最殊胜的供养。何谓法供养？不违背诸法、不舍离菩提心、以正见安住、如说修行，是名法供养。与其用无数财宝供养佛塔，不如受持读诵、为人解说一四句偈——后者是真正的法供养。

Indra asked the Buddha: among all forms of offering, which is supreme? The Buddha answered: the offering of Dharma — not contradicting the teaching, not abandoning bodhicitta, dwelling in right view, practising as taught — this is the supreme offering, exceeding all material offerings however vast

The concept of 'Dharma offering' (法供养) was introduced briefly in Chapter 4. Here it receives its full treatment. The contrast the sutra draws is between material offerings — however generous, however vast — and the offering of actually living the teaching. The first can be done without understanding or transformation; the second requires and produces both. A single verse of teaching received and practised is more valuable than oceans of treasure offered to countless stupas, because only the Dharma offering actually does the work that the Buddha-path is meant to do.

月盖王子——往昔本生中的法供养

佛陀举出本生故事：过去有一位月盖王子，为了求法，向药王如来请问。药王如来说：此经（即维摩诘所说经）是诸法之本，是不可思议解脱的源泉，是菩萨行的精要。月盖王子听后，发心护持此经，以法供养药王如来，后来成就道果。这一本生故事将本经的价值直接嵌入了一个历史性的叙事：持此经即是法供养。

The Buddha told a past-life story: in a former age, a prince named Moon Canopy asked the Buddha Medicine King about this very sutra; Medicine King Buddha praised the Vimalakirti Sutra as the source of all teachings, the essence of inconceivable liberation, and the core of the bodhisattva's practice; the prince made a Dharma offering of this sutra and later attained the path

The past-life frame serves a clear function: it legitimises the sutra itself. By having a past Buddha praise the Vimalakirti Sutra as the highest teaching, the text establishes its own canonical status. But the teaching is not merely self-promotional — the prince's story illustrates concretely what 'Dharma offering' means: hearing this teaching, being transformed by it, protecting and transmitting it, and living according to it. The offering is not passive reception but active engagement and propagation.

法供养的内容：如说修行，依法而住

佛陀详细说明了法供养的内容：如所说修行——依法而住，不违教义；信解深义而不以我见障之；不惜身命，护持正法；常精进、常慈悲、常以平等心接纳众生；不贪世间名利，不为诽谤所动；不以说法博取供养，只为众生得利益故说法。这是一份完整的「如何做法供养」的实践指南。

The Buddha elaborated: Dharma offering means practising what is taught, dwelling in accordance with the Dharma, not contradicting it with self-view, not sparing body or life to protect the true teaching, always being diligent and compassionate, not seeking fame or benefit from teaching, teaching only for the benefit of beings

This list of requirements for genuine Dharma offering is striking for what it demands: not the resources of a wealthy donor (gold, gems, elaborate ritual), but the quality of one's practice, motivation, and relationship to the teaching. The two items that recur most strongly are: practise what is taught (don't just know it), and teach only for beings' benefit (not for personal recognition). These are the tests of whether an offering of Dharma is genuine.

从迦罗鸠孙陀为始——七佛传承与此经的地位

佛陀说：此经（维摩诘所说经）从迦罗鸠孙陀佛（过去七佛之首）以来，历代皆以法供养流传，至今不断。这把本经置入了从远古诸佛延续至今的传承之中：持此经者，即是加入了一条跨越无量劫的「法供养」流传链。这对读者也是一种邀请：成为这条链中的一环。

The Buddha declared: this sutra has been transmitted through Dharma offering beginning from Krakucchanda (the first of the seven ancient Buddhas) down through all subsequent Buddhas to the present — those who uphold this sutra join an unbroken lineage of Dharma offering stretching across immeasurable kalpas

The mention of Krakucchanda — the first of the Seven Buddhas of Antiquity — places the sutra at the beginning of the current cosmic cycle. The claim is vast: this teaching has been in circulation, transmitted through Dharma offering, longer than the current cosmic age. To receive it now is to receive something that has survived immeasurable cycles of time through the dedication of countless beings. The invitation to uphold it is simultaneously humble (you are one link in a very long chain) and inspiring (the chain runs all the way back to the beginning).

以法供养比财供养——供养的真正意义

全章的结论：若以满三千大千世界七宝，供养无量诸佛，不如以四句偈受持读诵，为人解说——后者的功德超越前者无量无边。这不是贬低财供养，而是在说：财供养是外在的给予；法供养是内在的转化。一个改变了心性的人，其价值超过所有的外在供奉。佛陀最终所要的，是弟子成为觉悟的存在，而不是拥有大量财宝的施主。

The chapter's conclusion: filling the three-thousand-fold world with precious gems and offering them to countless Buddhas is surpassed by receiving, reciting, and explaining even four lines of this teaching — because material offering is external giving, while Dharma offering is internal transformation

The comparison is deliberately extreme to make the point unforgettable. It is not that material generosity is worthless — the sutra does not say this. It is that material generosity, however staggering in scale, does not in itself change the mind that performs it. The Dharma offering, by contrast, transforms the one who gives it and the one who receives it. The person who is genuinely changed by a teaching — who lives differently, sees more clearly, suffers less and causes less suffering — is a greater gift to the world than any amount of precious metals.

总结 · Summary

法供养品是全经对「供养」这一佛教核心实践的彻底重新定位。通过天帝的问题、佛陀的答复、月盖王子的本生故事和七佛传承的历史叙事，本品论证了一个核心命题：以法供养（如说修行、护持正法、为众生说法）的功德，超越一切财供养，无量无边。这一主题在经的第四品（善德长者子的斋会）就已埋下伏笔，至此全面展开。全章以对弟子的呼唤作结：受持此经即是法供养，而法供养是连接过去七佛到未来世界的一条不断的传承之链。

Chapter 13 is the sutra's definitive statement on the nature of offering. Through the question-and-answer between Indra and the Buddha, the past-life story of Prince Moon Canopy, and the lineage stretching back through the Seven Ancient Buddhas to Krakucchanda, the chapter argues that Dharma offering — living the teaching, protecting it, transmitting it — surpasses all material giving. The distinction is not between generosity and miserliness, but between external giving (which changes what you have) and internal transformation (which changes what you are). The chapter ends by weaving the reader into a transmission lineage that stretches across immeasurable time: to uphold this sutra now is to carry a flame that has been burning since the very first Buddha of this cosmic age.
