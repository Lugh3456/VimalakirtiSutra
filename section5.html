第五章 · 文殊师利问疾品

维摩诘所说经

← 第四章 目录 Contents 第六章 →

尔时佛告文殊师利。汝行诣维摩诘问疾。文殊师利白佛言。世尊。彼上人者难为詶对。深达实相善说法要。辩才无滞智慧无碍。一切菩萨法式悉知。诸佛秘藏无不得入降伏众魔游戏神通。其慧方便皆已得度。虽然当承佛圣旨诣彼问疾。于是众中诸菩萨大弟子释梵四天王等咸作是念。今二大士文殊师利维摩诘共谈。必说妙法。即时八千菩萨五百声闻。百千天人皆欲随从于是文殊师利与诸菩萨大弟子众及诸天人恭敬围绕入毘耶离大城尔时长者维摩诘心念。今文殊师利与大众俱来。即以神力空其室内。除去所有及诸侍者。唯置一床以疾而卧。文殊师利既入其舍。见其室空无诸所有独寝一床。时维摩诘言。善来文殊师利。不来相而来。不见相而见文殊师利言。如是居士。若来已更不来。若去已更不去。所以者何。来者无所从来去者无所至所可见者更不可见。且置是事。居士。是疾宁可忍不。疗治有损不至增乎。世尊慇懃致问无量。居士。是疾何所因起。其生久如。当云何灭。维摩诘言。从痴有爱则我病生。以一切众生病是故我病。若一切众生病灭则我病灭。所以者何。菩萨为众生故入生死。有生死则有病。若众生得离病者。则菩萨无复病。譬如长者唯有一子其子得病父母亦病。若子病愈父母亦愈。菩萨如是。于诸众生爱之若子。众生病则菩萨病。众生病愈菩萨亦愈。又言。是疾何所因起。菩萨病者以大悲起。文殊师利言。居士。此室何以空无侍者。维摩诘言。诸佛国土亦复皆空。又问。以何为空。答曰。以空空。又问。空何用空。答曰。以无分别空故空。又问。空可分别耶。答曰。分别亦空。又问。空当于何求。答曰。当于六十二见中求。又问。六十二见当于何求。答曰。当于诸佛解脱中求。又问。诸佛解脱当于何求。答曰。当于一切众生心行中求。又仁所问何无侍者。一切众魔及诸外道皆吾侍也。所以者何。众魔者乐生死。菩萨于生死而不舍。外道者乐诸见。菩萨于诸见而不动。文殊师利言。居士所疾。为何等相。维摩诘言。我病无形不可见。又问。此病身合耶心合耶。答曰。非身合身相离故。亦非心合心如幻故。又问。地大水大火大风大。于此四大何大之病。答曰。是病非地大亦不离地大。水火风大亦复如是。而众生病从四大起。以其有病是故我病。尔时文殊师利问维摩诘言。菩萨应云何慰喻有疾菩萨。维摩诘言。说身无常不说厌离于身。说身有苦不说乐于涅槃。说身无我而说教导众生。说身空寂不说毕竟寂灭。说悔先罪而不说入。于过去。以己之疾愍于彼疾。当识宿世无数劫苦。当念饶益一切众生忆所修福。念于净命。勿生忧恼常起精进。当作医王疗治众病。菩萨应如是慰喻有疾菩萨令其欢喜。文殊师利言。居士。有疾菩萨云何调伏其心。维摩诘言。有疾菩萨应作是念。今我此病皆从前世妄想颠倒诸烦恼生。无有实法谁受病者。所以者何。四大合故假名为身。四大无主身亦无我。又此病起皆由著我。是故于我不应生著。既知病本即除我想及众生想。当起法想。应作是念。但以众法合成此身。起唯法起灭唯法灭。又此法者各不相知。起时不言我起。灭时不言我灭。彼有疾菩萨为灭法想。当作是念。此法想者亦是颠倒。颠倒者是即大患。我应离之。云何为离。离我我所。云何离我我所。谓离二法。云何离二法。谓不念内外诸法行于平等。云何平等。为我等涅槃等。所以者何。我及涅槃此二皆空。以何为空。但以名字故空。如此二法无决定性。得是平等无有余病。唯有空病空病亦空。是有疾菩萨以无所受而受诸受。未具佛法亦不灭受而取证也。设身有苦念恶趣众生起大悲心。我既调伏亦当调伏一切众生。但除其病而不除法。为断病本而教导之。何谓病本。谓有攀缘。从有攀缘则为病本。何所攀缘谓之三界。云何断攀缘以无所得。若无所得则无攀缘。何谓无所得。谓离二见。何谓二见。谓内见外见是无所得。文殊师利。是为有疾菩萨调伏其心。为断老病死苦是菩萨菩提。若不如是己所修治为无慧利。譬如胜怨乃可为勇。如是兼除老病死者菩萨之谓也。彼有疾菩萨应复作是念。如我此病非真非有。众生病亦非真非有。作是观时。于诸众生若起爱见大悲。即应舍离。所以者何。菩萨断除客尘烦恼而起大悲。爱见悲者则于生死有疲厌心。若能离此无有疲厌。在在所生不为爱见之所覆也。所生无缚能为众生说法解缚。如佛所说。若自有缚能解彼缚无有是处。若自无缚。能解彼缚斯有是处。是故菩萨不应起缚。何谓缚何谓解。贪著禅味是菩萨缚。以方便生是菩萨解。又无方便慧缚。有方便慧解。无慧方便缚。有慧方便解。何谓无方便慧缚。谓菩萨以爱见心。庄严佛土成就众生。于空无相无作法中而自调伏。是名无方便慧缚。何谓有方便慧解。谓不以爱见心庄严佛土成就众生。于空无相无作法中。以自调伏而不疲厌。是名有方便慧解。何谓无慧方便缚。谓菩萨住贪欲瞋恚邪见等诸烦恼。而植众德本。是名无慧方便缚。何谓有慧方便解。谓离诸贪欲瞋恚邪见等诸烦恼。而植众德本。回向阿耨多罗三藐三菩提。是名有慧方便解。文殊师利。彼有疾菩萨应如是观诸法。又复观身无常苦空非我。是名为慧。虽身有疾常在生死。饶益一切而不厌倦。是名方便。又复观身身不离病病不离身。是病是身非新非故。是名为慧。设身有疾而不永灭。是名方便。文殊师利。有疾菩萨应如是调伏其心不住其中。亦复不住不调伏心。所以者何。若住不调伏心是愚人法。若住调伏心是声闻法。是故菩萨不当住于调伏不调伏心。离此二法是菩萨行。在于生死不为污行。住于涅槃不永灭度。是菩萨行。非凡夫行非贤圣行。是菩萨行。非垢行非净行。是菩萨行。虽过魔行。而现降众魔。是菩萨行。求一切智无非时求。是菩萨行。虽观诸法不生而不入正位。是菩萨行。虽观十二缘起而入诸邪见。是菩萨行。虽摄一切众生而不爱著。是菩萨行。虽乐远离而不依身心尽。是菩萨行。虽行三界而不坏法性。是菩萨行。虽行于空而植众德本。是菩萨行。虽行无相而度众生。是菩萨行。虽行无作而现受身。是菩萨行。虽行无起而起一切善行。是菩萨行。虽行六波罗蜜而遍知众生心心数法。是菩萨行。虽行六通而不尽漏。是菩萨行。虽行四无量心而不贪著生于梵世。是菩萨行。虽行禅定解脱三昧而不随禅生。是菩萨行。虽行四念处而不永离身受心法。是菩萨行。虽行四正勤而不舍身心精进。是菩萨行。虽行四如意足而得自在神通。是菩萨行。虽行五根而分别众生诸根利钝。是菩萨行。虽行五力而乐求佛十力。是菩萨行。虽行七觉分而分别佛之智慧。是菩萨行。虽行八圣道而乐行无量佛道。是菩萨行。虽行止观助道之法而不毕竟堕于寂灭。是菩萨行。虽行诸法不生不灭而以相好庄严其身。是菩萨行。虽现声闻辟支佛威仪而不舍佛法。是菩萨行。虽随诸法究竟净相而随所应为现其身。是菩萨行。虽观诸佛国土永寂如空而现种种清净佛土。是菩萨行。虽得佛道转于法轮入于涅槃而不舍于菩萨之道。是菩萨行。说是语时文殊师利所将大众。其中八千天子皆发阿耨多罗三藐三菩提心

逐句解释

今二大士共谈，必说妙法——文殊入室

经过两品的铺垫，文殊师利终于接受佛命，前往探视维摩诘。消息一出，八千菩萨、五百声闻、百千天人全都随从而来。维摩诘早知文殊将至，以神力将室内一切清空，只剩自己卧于一床。空室的景象本身就是一个教法：一切执著，包括对空间和所有物的执著，都已放下。

When word spread that Manjushri was going to visit Vimalakirti, 8,000 bodhisattvas, 500 shravakas and hundreds of thousands of gods all followed — Vimalakirti had already emptied his room of everything except himself lying on one bed

The great conversation is about to begin, and the whole assembly wants to watch. Vimalakirti's preparation is characteristic: he clears the room entirely. No furniture, no attendants, nothing — just the empty space and a man lying in bed. The empty room is already a teaching before a single word is spoken. It enacts the non-clinging that will be discussed throughout the chapter. The greatest dialogue in Mahayana literature begins in a room that has been emptied of everything.

不来相而来，不见相而见——文殊与维摩诘的第一句话

文殊进门，维摩诘说：善来文殊师利！不来相而来，不见相而见。文殊回应：如是居士。若来已更不来，若去已更不去。这两句对话浓缩了整章的义理：真正的来去不在相上——没有一个实体在移动，没有一个见者在观看。这是对来去、见闻等基本认知结构的当下解构。

Vimalakirti greeted Manjushri: 'Well come, Manjushri — you come without the mark of coming, and see without the mark of seeing.' Manjushri replied: 'Just so — one who has come no longer comes, one who has gone no longer goes'

The opening exchange is extraordinary for its density. 'Without the mark of coming' means: there is no fixed entity called 'Manjushri' that has been located elsewhere and has now relocated here. The movement is conventional; the mover is not ultimately real. Manjushri's response mirrors this: once someone has arrived, the arriving is complete — there is no ongoing process of 'coming' that persists. This mutual recognition between the two figures establishes them as equals and signals that the conversation will operate at the level of ultimate reality, not conventional pleasantry.

维摩诘之疾从何所来——疾病的根源与菩提

文殊问：居士之疾，从何所起，其生久如，当云何灭？维摩诘答：从痴有爱，则我病生。以一切众生病，是故我病；若一切众生得不病者，则我病灭。菩萨之疾，为大悲起。这是本章最关键的一段：维摩诘的疾病不是个人的业报，而是与众生同悲共苦的大悲心的表现——众生不愈，菩萨不愈。

Manjushri asked: from what does your illness arise, and how is it to be cured? Vimalakirti answered: from delusion comes craving, and from that my illness arises; my illness comes from the illness of all beings; if all beings were free of illness, my illness would cease — the bodhisattva's illness arises from great compassion

This is the chapter's doctrinal heart and one of the sutra's most beautiful moments. Vimalakirti's illness is not his own — it is borrowed from the suffering of every being he has vowed to liberate. The bodhisattva cannot be well in a world where beings are suffering; that would be a kind of spiritual selfishness. His sickness is the form that great compassion (mahakaruna) takes when it is completely genuine. The cure is not personal healing but the liberation of all beings. This redefines illness itself: the most enlightened being in the room is the one who is sick, and he is sick precisely because of how deeply he cares.

菩萨应如何慰喻有疾菩萨——以空慰病

文殊进一步问：如何安慰生病的菩萨？维摩诘答：当观身无常、苦、空、非我。观身有病，一切众生亦有病，不应起悲念，又当起除众病想。应灭身中诸苦痛，即念彼众生苦恼。要如实观身中病：不生不起，不来不去，非内非外，不在中间——这才是真正的以法安慰。

Manjushri asked how to console a sick bodhisattva; Vimalakirti said: contemplate the body as impermanent, suffering, empty, and not-self; contemplate that all beings too are sick; do not generate sentimental grief but resolve to remove the diseases of all; observe the illness without grasping — not arising, not ceasing, not coming, not going, not inside, not outside

The advice is not sentimental consolation but a precise meditation practice. The first move is standard Buddhist reflection on the body's impermanence and emptiness — no different from what any monk would teach. But then Vimalakirti adds the bodhisattva dimension: extend this observation to all beings simultaneously. The sick bodhisattva should not wallow in personal suffering or be consoled out of it, but should use the experience of their own illness as a direct gateway into the shared suffering of all beings. Pain becomes a training ground for compassion rather than an obstacle to it.

维摩诘之疾的究竟：不生不灭，离于诸相

维摩诘说：有疾菩萨，应作是念——今我此病，皆从前世妄想颠倒诸烦恼生。……又当知病从缘起，缘起即无定性，空。于空性中，无病可得，无身可病。这把疾病最终还原为空性的问题：既然身体本空，则病也是空；既然病是空，则以空慰病，是真正的安慰。

Vimalakirti teaches: the sick bodhisattva should reflect that this illness arises from delusion and craving in former lives; all conditioned things arise from conditions and have no fixed nature; in emptiness there is no illness to be found, and no body to be ill

The chapter closes the circle opened by Vimalakirti's answer about his own illness. If illness arises from craving and delusion, and if craving and delusion arise from the mistaken belief in a fixed self, then the deepest cure is the direct insight into the emptiness of self and body. This doesn't mean denying the experience of pain — Vimalakirti is very much lying in bed. It means seeing through the framework that makes pain feel like a permanent, personal affliction rather than a conditioned, impersonal arising that will pass.

以无所受而受诸受——不断不常，中道之法

文殊与维摩诘的对话到此进入最深处：以无所受而受诸受，不断不常，不苦不乐，不增不减，不生不灭，不去不来。在这一切否定中，维摩诘展示了一条中道：既不执著于感受（无所受），又不逃离感受（而受诸受）。这是行中道而不堕两边的核心修行要领。

Vimalakirti teaches: receive all feelings with non-receiving — not cutting off and not permanent, not painful and not pleasant, not increasing and not decreasing, not arising and not ceasing, not going and not coming

This sequence of paired negations is the chapter's philosophical climax. Each pair points to the middle way between two extremes: between denial (cutting off feeling) and indulgence (being swept away by feeling); between grasping for pleasure and fleeing from pain. The phrase 'receive all feelings with non-receiving' is a paradox that works in practice: the bodhisattva does not suppress the experience of being ill, but also does not add to the raw sensation the layer of personal narrative (I am suffering, this is wrong, it should be different). What remains when that layer is removed is bare experience — and bare experience, the sutra suggests, is neither painful nor pleasant in the absolute sense.

维摩诘的空室与大众随从——不思议解脱的伏笔

文殊带来了众多菩萨和大众，而维摩诘的室内只有一张床。这个对比——人满为患的随行与一空如洗的室内——已经在暗示下一章「不思议品」将要展示的主题：无量的宇宙可以容入一粒芥子，无数的大众可以居于一间空室。这是全经「以小容大、以少容多」奇迹的第一个预示。

Manjushri's entourage of thousands entered a room that contained only one bed and one man — this contrast already foreshadows the miracle of the next chapter, where Mt Sumeru fits inside a mustard seed and infinite space folds into an empty room

The spatial paradox of Chapter 5 — a vast crowd entering a tiny empty room — is never explicitly commented on in this chapter, but it sets up the 'inconceivable liberation' of Chapter 6. The sutra is laying groundwork here: if you have already accepted that the emptied room can hold all these people without crowding, you are being prepared for the far larger claims about space and matter that are coming. Vimalakirti's room is a kind of training space for the impossible.

总结 · Summary

文殊师利问疾品是全经的情感和哲学高峰之一。两位大士的相遇，从第一句话（「不来相而来，不见相而见」）便已在究竟层面展开。维摩诘之疾的根源是大悲心——因众生有病而病，因众生不愈而不愈。对病中菩萨的安慰不是世俗的同情，而是以空性观、以中道行来彻底瓦解「身」「病」「苦」的实在感。章末「以无所受而受诸受」，是全经对苦的最深回应：既不逃避，也不执著，在受与不受之间，守住中道。

Chapter 5 is one of the sutra's emotional and philosophical peaks. The meeting of Manjushri and Vimalakirti begins at the level of ultimate reality from the very first exchange. Vimalakirti's illness is revealed to be an act of compassion: he is sick because all beings are sick, and he will not recover until they do. The teaching on consoling a sick bodhisattva turns conventional sympathy into a meditation practice: observe impermanence, observe emptiness, extend the observation to all beings simultaneously, and receive experience with non-receiving. The chapter closes with the paradox at the heart of the middle way — 'receive all feelings with non-receiving' — which is not a formula for emotional suppression but a description of awareness that neither grasps nor rejects what arises.
