第十四章 · 嘱累品

维摩诘所说经

← 第十三章 目录 Contents

于是佛告弥勒菩萨言。弥勒。我今以是无量亿阿僧祇劫所集阿耨多罗三藐三菩提法。付嘱于汝。如是辈经于佛灭后末世之中。汝等当以神力广宣流布于阎浮提无令断绝。所以者何。未来世中当有善男子善女人。及天龙鬼神干闼婆罗刹等。发阿耨多罗三藐三菩提心乐于大法。若使不闻如是等经则失善利。如此辈人闻是等经。必多信乐发希有心当以顶受随诸众生所应得利而为广说。弥勒当知。菩萨有二相。何谓为二。一者好于杂句文饰之事。二者不畏深义如实能入。若好杂句文饰事者。当知是为新学菩萨。若于如是无染无著甚深经典。无有恐畏能入其中。闻已心净受持读诵如说修行。当知是为久修道行。弥勒。复有二法。名新学者。不能决定于甚深法。何等为二。一者所未闻深经。闻之惊怖生疑不能随顺。毁谤不信而作是言。我初不闻从何所来。二者若有护持解说如是深经者。不肯亲近供养恭敬。或时于中说其过恶。有此二法。当知是为新学菩萨。为自毁伤。不能于深法中调伏其心。弥勒。复有二法。菩萨虽信解深法。犹自毁伤而不能得无生法忍。何等为二。一者轻慢新学菩萨而不教诲。二者虽解深法而取相分别。是为二法弥勒菩萨闻说是已白佛言。世尊。未曾有也。如佛所说。我当远离如斯之恶奉持如来无数阿僧祇劫所集阿耨多罗三藐三菩提法。若未来世善男子善女人求大乘者当令手得如是等经。与其念力。使受持读诵为他广说。世尊。若后末世有能受持读诵为他说者。当知皆是弥勒神力之所建立。佛言。善哉善哉弥勒。如汝所说。佛助尔喜于是一切菩萨合掌白佛。我等亦于如来灭后。十方国土广宣流布阿耨多罗三藐三菩提法。复当开导诸说法者令得是经尔时四天王白佛言。世尊。在在处处城邑聚落山林旷野。有是经卷读诵解说者。我当率诸官属为听法故往诣其所拥护其人。面百由旬令无伺求得其便者是时佛告阿难。受持是经广宣流布。阿难言唯然。我已受持要者。世尊。当何名斯经。佛言。阿难。是经名为维摩诘所说。亦名不可思议解脱法门。如是受持。佛说是经已。长者维摩诘．文殊师利．舍利弗．阿难等。及诸天人阿修罗一切大众。闻佛所说皆大欢喜维摩诘经卷下

逐句解释

佛嘱阿难受持此经——传法的庄严

经文结束前，佛陀郑重嘱托阿难：受持此经，广令流布。阿难问：此经名何？佛陀答：此经名维摩诘所说，亦名不可思议解脱。以此两名受持之。这是全经的正式命名时刻，也是将此经的传播责任正式托付给阿难——作为佛陀记忆的守护者和一切经文的持守者。

Before the sutra ends, the Buddha solemnly entrusted Ananda with the responsibility of preserving and propagating this sutra; Ananda asked for the title; the Buddha answered: this sutra is called 'The Teaching of Vimalakirti' and also 'Inconceivable Liberation' — receive and uphold it under these names

The formal naming of the sutra is an important ritual moment. Throughout the text, the teaching has been unfolding without being explicitly named. Now, at the close, it receives two names — one named after its speaker (Vimalakirti), one named after its central theme (Inconceivable Liberation). The two names are complementary: the first anchors the teaching in a specific person and setting; the second points to the universal principle that the person and setting have been illustrating. Ananda — whose perfect memory preserved all the Buddha's teachings — is the appropriate guardian.

弥勒菩萨发愿护持此经——未来的传承

弥勒菩萨在大众中发愿：我当护持此经，使其在未来末法时代流布不绝。弥勒即未来佛，他的发愿意味着：这部经将跨越从当前佛陀到下一个佛陀的整个时段，持续发挥其作用。这把本经的传播时间线，从当下一直延伸到弥勒世界——时间上的无尽延续，对应了第六章空间上的无限容纳。

Maitreya Bodhisattva vowed to protect and propagate this sutra in the future, including through the age of decline into the time of the next Buddha — pledging an unbroken transmission from the present through to the age of Maitreya

Maitreya's vow is structurally significant: it bridges the gap between the present age and the next Buddha's age, a span of almost inconceivable length in Buddhist cosmology. His taking of responsibility for the sutra's transmission is a guarantee written into the fabric of the future. The sutra is not just for the present assembly in Vimalakirti's room; it is for every age, every difficulty, every configuration of beings who will need to hear that the inconceivable liberation is available.

大众闻法，各得其益——万人受化，诸天散花

大众听闻经法，各有所得：无量天人发阿耨多罗三藐三菩提心，无量菩萨得无生法忍，无量声闻断诸漏尽，无量众生乃至禽兽皆得不退转于道。诸天散宝花于大众，作最后的庄严供养。这是对一场法会的全景性总结：法音所及之处，任何层次的众生都有所受益，没有人（也没有任何存在）被遗漏。

The assembly heard the teaching and each received a corresponding benefit: countless devas aroused bodhicitta, countless bodhisattvas attained acceptance of non-arising, countless disciples extinguished all defilements, countless beings including animals attained non-regression on the path — and the gods scattered jewelled flowers as a final offering

The sutra closes with an image of total, inclusive transformation. Every tier of the assembly — gods, bodhisattvas, monks, lay practitioners, and even animals — receives a benefit calibrated to their capacity. This is the final expression of upaya (skillful means): a single teaching event that simultaneously addresses every level of spiritual development present. The gods scattering flowers echoes the goddess's flower-scattering in Chapter 7 — but now without the test and without the flowers sticking. The assembly has been transformed.

嘱累品的意义——经末嘱托与传法的责任

「嘱累」的意思是「郑重嘱托，托付传承」。本品是整部经的正式结尾：佛陀将此经托付给阿难和弥勒，令其广为流布，不令断绝。这个嘱托结构本身就是一个教法：法的传承需要人的担承，没有一个「自动流传」的机制。每一个听闻此经的人，都成为了这个嘱托链条中的一环——包括现在正在阅读这些文字的你。

The title 嘱累 means 'solemn entrustment and transmission' — the formal act of passing responsibility for the teaching to future bearers; the Buddha's entrustment to Ananda and Maitreya is itself a teaching: the Dharma requires human beings to carry it forward; every person who receives this teaching becomes a link in the chain of entrustment

The final chapter's title is its teaching. 嘱累 — solemn entrustment — names the act of transmission that makes any teaching survive. The Buddha's enlightenment does not automatically propagate itself through the universe; it requires practitioners who receive it, are transformed by it, and pass it on. The sutra ends not with a philosophical conclusion but with an act of delegation: go and carry this. Ananda, Maitreya, and every being who has heard this teaching is now responsible for it. The question the sutra leaves the reader with is not 'do you understand inconceivable liberation?' but 'what will you do with it?'

总结 · Summary

嘱累品是全经的正式结尾，以佛陀对阿难的嘱托开始，以诸天散花、大众受益作结。经的两个名字——「维摩诘所说经」和「不可思议解脱」——在此正式宣布。弥勒菩萨发愿护持此经，将其传承延伸至下一个佛陀的时代。而大众的受益场景，则是对全经核心主旨的最后一次确认：方便无量、应机各异，但法之利益遍及一切。最后，嘱累这一行为本身就是教法：法的流传不是自动的，需要每一个听闻者的担承与传递。

Chapter 14 brings the sutra to a formal close with three acts: naming (the Buddha gives the sutra its two titles), entrustment (Maitreya vows to protect and transmit it across the entire gap between this age and the next), and fruition (the assembly is transformed at every level, from the highest bodhisattvas to animals). The final image of gods scattering flowers echoes the goddess episode in Chapter 7, but now without division — the entire assembly is present, and the flowers fall freely. The sutra's last word is not a philosophical statement but an act of delegation: the teaching has been given, and now each person who has received it bears responsibility for it. The inconceivable liberation is not a private possession; it is something to be lived, transmitted, and given away.
