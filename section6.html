第六章 · 不思议品

维摩诘所说经

← 第五章 目录 Contents 第七章 →

尔时舍利弗。见此室中无有床座。作是念。斯诸菩萨大弟子众当于何坐。长者维摩诘知其意。语舍利弗言。云何仁者。为法来耶求床座耶。舍利弗言。我为法来非为床座。维摩诘言。唯舍利弗。夫求法者不贪躯命。何况床座。夫求法者。非有色受想行识之求。非有界入之求。非有欲色无色之求。唯舍利弗。夫求法者。不著佛求不著法求不著众求。夫求法者。无见苦求无断集求。无造尽证修道之求。所以者何。法无戏论。若言我当见苦断集证灭修道。是则戏论非求法也。唯舍利弗。法名寂灭。若行生灭是求生灭非求法也。法名无染。若染于法乃至涅槃。是则染著非求法也。法无行处。若行于法是则行处非求法也。法无取舍。若取舍法是则取舍非求法也。法无处所。若著处所。是则著处非求法也。法名无相。若随相识是则求相非求法也。法不可住。若住于法是则住法非求法也。法不可见闻觉知。若行见闻觉知。是则见闻觉知非求法也。法名无为。若行有为是求有为非求法也。是故舍利弗。若求法者。于一切法应无所求。说是语时。五百天子于诸法中得法眼净尔时长者维摩诘问文殊师利。仁者。游于无量千万亿阿僧祇国。何等佛土有好上妙功德成就师子之座。文殊师利言。居士。东方度三十六河沙国有世界。名须弥相。其佛号须弥灯王。今现在。彼佛身长八万四千由旬。其师子座高八万四千由旬严饰第一。于是长者维摩诘。现神通力。即时彼佛遣三万二千师子座高广严净。来入维摩诘室。诸菩萨大弟子释梵四天王等昔所未见。其室广博悉皆包容三万二千师子座。无所妨碍。于毘耶离城及阎浮提四天下。亦不迫迮。悉见如故尔时维摩诘语文殊师利。就师子座。与诸菩萨上人俱坐。当自立身如彼座像。其得神通菩萨即自变形。为四万二千由旬坐师子座。诸新发意菩萨及大弟子皆不能升尔时维摩诘语舍利弗。就师子座。舍利弗言。居士。此座高广吾不能升。维摩诘言。唯舍利弗。为须弥灯王如来作礼乃可得坐。于是新发意菩萨及大弟子。即为须弥灯王如来作礼。便得坐师子座。舍利弗言。居士未曾有也。如是小室乃容受此高广之座。于毘耶离城无所妨碍。又于阎浮提聚落城邑及四天下诸天龙王鬼神宫殿。亦不迫迮。维摩诘言。唯舍利弗。诸佛菩萨有解脱名不可思议。若菩萨住是解脱者。以须弥之高广内芥子中无所增减。须弥山王本相如故。而四天王忉利诸天。不觉不知己之所入。唯应度者乃见须弥入芥子中。是名住不思议解脱法门。又以四大海水入一毛孔。不娆鱼鳖鼋鼍水性之属。而彼大海本相如故。诸龙鬼神阿修罗等不觉不知己之所入。于此众生亦无所娆。又舍利弗。住不可思议解脱菩萨。断取三千大千世界。如陶家轮著右掌中。掷过河沙世界之外。其中众生不觉不知己之所往。又复还置本处。都不使人有往来想。而此世界本相如故。又舍利弗。或有众生乐久住世而可度者。菩萨即延七日以为一劫。令彼众生谓之一劫。或有众生不乐久住而可度者。菩萨即促一劫以为七日。令彼众生谓之七日。又舍利弗。住不可思议解脱菩萨。以一切佛土严饰之事。集在一国示于众生。又菩萨以一佛土众生置之右掌。飞到十方遍示一切。而不动本处。又舍利弗十方众生供养诸佛之具。菩萨于一毛孔皆令得见。又十方国土所有日月星宿。于一毛孔普使见之。又舍利弗。十方世界所有诸风。菩萨悉能吸著口中而身无损。外诸树木亦不摧折。又十方世界劫尽烧时。以一切火内于腹中。火事如故而不为害。又于下方过河沙等诸佛世界。取一佛土举著上方。过河沙无数世界。如持针锋举一枣叶而无所娆。又舍利弗。住不可思议解脱菩萨。能以神通现作佛身。或现辟支佛身。或现声闻身。或现帝释身。或现梵王身。或现世主身。或现转轮王身。又十方世界所有众声。上中下音皆能变之令作佛声。演出无常苦空无我之音。及十方诸佛所说种种之法。皆于其中。普令得闻。舍利弗。我今略说菩萨不可思议解脱之力。若广说者穷劫不尽。是时大迦叶。闻说菩萨不可思议解脱法门。叹未曾有。谓舍利弗。譬如有人于盲者前现众色像非彼所见。一切声闻闻是不可思议解脱法门。不能解了为若此也。智者闻是。其谁不发阿耨多罗三藐三菩提心。我等何为永绝其根。于此大乘已如败种。一切声闻闻是不可思议解脱法门。皆应号泣声震三千大千世界。一切菩萨应大欣庆顶受此法。若有菩萨信解不可思议解脱法门者。一切魔众无如之何。大迦叶说是语时。三万二千天子皆发阿耨多罗三藐三菩提心尔时维摩诘语大迦叶。仁者。十方无量阿僧祇世界中作魔王者。多是住不可思议解脱菩萨。以方便力教化众生现作魔王。又迦叶。十方无量菩萨。或有人从乞手足耳鼻头目髓脑血肉皮骨聚落城邑妻子奴婢象马车乘金银琉璃车磲马瑙珊瑚琥珀真珠珂贝衣服饮食。如此乞者多是住不可思议解脱菩萨。以方便力而往试之令其坚固。所以者何。住不可思议解脱菩萨。有威德力故现行逼迫。示诸众生如是难事。凡夫下劣无有力势。不能如是逼迫菩萨。譬如龙象蹴踏非驴所堪。是名住不可思议解脱菩萨智慧方便之门

逐句解释

舍利弗无座——求法者不求床座

大众随文殊进入维摩诘的空室后，舍利弗注意到室内无座，心想众人将坐何处。维摩诘察觉，说：舍利弗，汝为法来耶？求床座耶？求法者不贪躯命，何况床座。这是本章的引子：对有形之物的执著，是对「法」的误解。

When the great assembly entered Vimalakirti's empty room, Shariputra worried about where everyone would sit; Vimalakirti said: did you come for the Dharma or for a seat? One who seeks the Dharma does not cling even to their own life, let alone to furniture

The scene opens with a small, almost comic concern: where will everyone sit? Vimalakirti turns it into a lesson. The Dharma is not a commodity that requires a proper setting. Seeking the Dharma means releasing attachment to comfort and convenience. The one who truly seeks the teaching is indifferent to whether they are sitting, standing, or lying down. This exchange is the doorway into the chapter's central theme: the 'inconceivable liberation,' which transcends all physical constraints.

维摩诘借三万二千师子座——须弥灯王佛的宝座

为了让大众就坐，维摩诘以神力从遥远的须弥灯王佛国借来三万二千宝师子座，高广庄严，置于室内，而室内空间竟毫不局促，毗耶离城也未曾变小。大众安坐其上，如入本国。这就是「不思议」的首次具体展示：有限的空间容纳了无量的宝座。

Vimalakirti used his supernatural powers to bring 32,000 magnificent lion thrones from the distant Buddha-land of Sumeru Lamp King and placed them in his room — yet the room was not crowded and the city of Vaishali was not enlarged

This is the chapter's opening miracle, and it is carefully designed to be impossible by any conventional understanding of space. 32,000 enormous thrones in one small room, with no crowding. The sutra does not offer an explanation — it simply says this is the 'inconceivable liberation' (不思议解脱) that advanced bodhisattvas attain. The inconceivable is not a magic trick; it is the consequence of having fully realised that spatial extension is a conventional description overlaid on a reality that is not inherently extended.

须弥山入芥子中——大小互容的不思议

维摩诘说：住不思议解脱菩萨，以须弥之高广，内芥子中，无所增减。须弥山王、四天王、忉利诸天，不觉不知己之所入，而应度脱者乃能见之。大小互容而各不相知，这是不思议解脱最惊人的表述：真正的自由超越了物理尺度的限制。

Vimalakirti explains: a bodhisattva who has attained inconceivable liberation can place the great Mt Sumeru inside a mustard seed without diminishing the mountain or enlarging the seed — and those dwelling inside neither know nor feel anything different, while those who are ready to be liberated can see it

Mt Sumeru (须弥山) is the axis mundi of Buddhist cosmology — the enormous mountain at the centre of the universe, vastly larger than any physical feature we can imagine. A mustard seed is the smallest object commonly cited in Buddhist texts. Vimalakirti claims that the bodhisattva in inconceivable liberation can fit the former inside the latter without changing either. This is not a claim about physical compression; it is a claim about the ultimate nature of spatial magnitude, which is conventionally attributed but not ultimately real. The mountain inside the seed and the seed that holds the mountain are both equally present, neither diminished nor enlarged.

四大海水入一毛孔——时间与空间的互融

不思议解脱的菩萨还能：以四大海水入一毛孔，不娆鱼鳖鼋鼍水性之属；摄取一个三千大千世界如陶家轮；将一劫延长为一日，或将一日压缩为一劫；以七宝充满恒河沙数世界，再将其置于一毛孔中。时间和空间的边界在这里彻底崩解。

The bodhisattva of inconceivable liberation can place all the water of the four great oceans into a single pore without disturbing the fish; can take an entire universe and spin it like a potter's wheel; can extend one kalpa into a single day or compress a day into a kalpa

The examples of inconceivable liberation keep escalating. The key in each case is that conventional relationships between large and small, long and short, are maintained from within the conventional framework, while being completely transcended from the perspective of the bodhisattva. The fish are not disturbed because, within their experiential reality, the ocean is unchanged. The inhabitants of the universe do not feel themselves being spun. The miracle is invisible to those who are not ready to see it — which is itself a teaching about the nature of perception.

以四天下置右掌中——诸佛神力与菩萨的不思议

维摩诘举例：十方诸佛的神力变化（如来秘密之法）也属不思议解脱的范畴。如以右掌取四天下，掷过恒河沙数世界之外；将一切众生安置于掌中，如陶家捻土；用一根毛发提起一个大海，沧海桑田不在话下。这些神通的意义不是炫技，而是说明心性可以超越所有物质的束缚。

Further examples: the bodhisattva can take the entire world-system in the right hand and throw it beyond countless Buddha-lands; can carry all beings in the palm of the hand; can lift an ocean with a single hair — none of these acts disturb the beings within

Each example in this catalogue of inconceivable powers points to the same underlying truth: from the perspective of ultimate reality, the distinctions between large and small, near and far, heavy and light are conventional labels, not absolute features. A being who has fully realised this is not constrained by those labels. The sutra is not recommending these as practices to aim for; it is using them as vivid images to dislodge the reader's assumption that physical scale is an absolute fact about reality.

维摩诘室能容此众——文殊叹不思议

文殊师利赞叹维摩诘的室内容纳了如此众多的菩萨、大弟子和神众，却丝毫不觉狭小。维摩诘说：十方诸佛菩萨有不思议解脱法门，以此故能于方寸之地演无边法。学道者当于此处生信解，不应以常情测量诸佛菩萨之境界。

Manjushri marvelled that Vimalakirti's room held such a vast assembly without feeling crowded; Vimalakirti said: those who have attained inconceivable liberation can teach boundless Dharma in a single square inch — practitioners should have faith in this rather than measuring the Buddha's realm by ordinary standards

The chapter turns reflexive here: the miracle being described is the very miracle that has been happening since the chapter began. Vimalakirti's room has been holding thousands of people. The sutra is pointing at its own structure. The 'inconceivable liberation' is not only a teaching subject — it is the condition of possibility for the text itself. And Vimalakirti's final point is practical: do not measure the Buddha's reality by the conventions of ordinary experience. Faith in the inconceivable is itself a form of liberation.

解脱有二：有为解脱与无为解脱——真正的自由超越形式

本品以一个关键的辩证结尾：维摩诘问文殊，何为解脱？文殊回答：若无缚无解，是为解脱。真正的解脱不是从某种束缚中逃出来，因为「束缚」本身是我们加诸实相的一种叙述。若能见到实相本无缚，便无需解脱，而这本身就是解脱。

Vimalakirti asks Manjushri about liberation; Manjushri replies: where there is neither bondage nor release, that is liberation — true freedom is not the escaping from a constraint, because the constraint itself is a description we impose on reality

The chapter's final teaching brings the inconceivable back to the foundational. All the miracles of space and time dissolving were illustrations of a single point: ultimate reality is not constrained by the conventional frameworks we use to describe it. Liberation is not about breaking free from chains — it is about seeing that the chains were always our own description, applied to a reality that was never actually bound. Where there is no bondage, there is no need for release. And that — the direct recognition of reality as it is — is what the sutra means by liberation.

总结 · Summary

不思议品是全经最富视觉冲击力的一章。从无座而来的三万二千师子座，到须弥山入芥子中，到四大海水入一毛孔——每一个例子都在挑战我们对空间、时间和物质的常识性理解。维摩诘借此展示「不思议解脱」的境界：证得此境的菩萨不受物理尺度的束缚，因为他们直接看见了物理尺度本身只是实相的一种约定性描述。全章以「无缚无解即解脱」作结，将所有的神通收归到最简洁的一句话：若你能见到实相本无束缚，这本身就是解脱。

Chapter 6 is the sutra's most visually spectacular. From thirty-two thousand lion thrones appearing in an empty room, to Mount Sumeru fitting inside a mustard seed, to the four great oceans entering a single pore — each example pushes further against our conventional understanding of physical reality. The term 'inconceivable liberation' (不思议解脱) names the attainment of bodhisattvas who have realised that spatial extension, temporal duration, and physical scale are conventional descriptions overlaid on a reality that is not inherently any of these things. The chapter ends by folding the spectacular back into the simple: true liberation is not escaping from bondage but recognising that reality was never bound. Where there is no constraint, there is nothing to be liberated from — and that recognition is itself freedom.
