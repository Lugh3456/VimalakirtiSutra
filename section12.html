第十二章 · 见阿閦佛品

维摩诘所说经

← 第十一章 目录 Contents 第十三章 →

尔时世尊问维摩诘。汝欲见如来。为以何等观如来乎。维摩诘言。如自观身实相。观佛亦然。我观如来。前际不来后际不去今则不住。不观色不观色如。不观色性。不观受想行识。不观识如。不观识性。非四大起。同于虚空。六入无积。眼耳鼻舌身心已过不在三界。三垢已离顺三脱门。具足三明与无明等。不一相不异相。不自相不他相。非无相非取相。不此岸不彼岸不中流。而化众生。观于寂灭亦不永灭。不此不彼。不以此不以彼。不可以智知。不可以识识。无晦无明无名无相。无强无弱非净非秽。不在方不离方。非有为非无为。无示无说。不施不悭。不戒不犯。不忍不恚。不进不怠。不定不乱。不智不愚。不诚不欺。不来不去。不出不入。一切言语道断。非福田非不福田。非应供养非不应供养。非取非舍。非有相非无相。同真际等法性。不可称不可量。过诸称量。非大非小。非见非闻非觉非知。离众结缚。等诸智同众生。于诸法无分别。一切无失。无浊无恼。无作无起无生无灭。无畏无忧无喜无厌无著。无已有无当有无今有。不可以一切言说分别显示。世尊。如来身为若此。作如是观。以斯观者名为正观。若他观者名为邪观尔时舍利弗问维摩诘。汝于何没而来生此。维摩诘言。汝所得法有没生乎。舍利弗言。无没生也。若诸法无没生相。云何问言汝于何没而来生此。于意云何。譬如幻师幻作男女。宁没生耶。舍利弗言。无没生也。汝岂不闻佛说诸法如幻相乎。答曰如是。若一切法如幻相者。云何问言汝于何没而来生此。舍利弗。没者为虚诳法败坏之相。生者为虚诳法相续之相。菩萨虽没不尽善本。虽生不长诸恶。是时佛告舍利弗。有国名妙喜。佛号无动。是维摩诘于彼国没而来生此。舍利弗言。未曾有也。世尊。是人乃能舍清净土。而来乐此多怒害处。维摩诘语舍利弗。于意云何。日光出时与冥合乎。答曰。不也。日光出时即无众冥。维摩诘言。夫日何故行阎浮提。答曰欲以明照为之除冥。维摩诘言。菩萨如是。虽生不净佛土为化众生故不与愚暗而共合也。但灭众生烦恼暗耳。是时大众渴仰。欲见妙喜世界无动如来及其菩萨声闻之众。佛知一切众会所念。告维摩诘言。善男子。为此众会。现妙喜国无动如来及诸菩萨声闻之众。众皆欲见。于是维摩诘心念。吾当不起于座接妙喜国。铁围山川溪谷江河。大海泉源须弥诸山。及日月星宿。天龙鬼神梵天等宫。并诸菩萨声闻之众。城邑聚落男女大小。乃至无动如来及菩提树诸妙莲华。能于十方作佛事者。三道宝阶从阎浮提至忉利天以此宝阶诸天来下。悉为礼敬无动如来听受经法。阎浮提人。亦登其阶。上升忉利见彼诸天。妙喜世界成就如是无量功德上至阿迦腻咤天。下至水际。以右手断取如陶家轮。入此世界犹持华鬘示一切众。作是念已入于三昧现神通力。以其右手断取妙喜世界置于此土。彼得神通菩萨及声闻众并余天人。俱发声言。唯然世尊。谁取我去。愿见救护。无动佛言。非我所为。是维摩诘神力所作。其余未得神通者。不觉不知己之所往。妙喜世界虽入此土而不增减。于是世界亦不迫隘如本无异尔时释迦牟尼佛告诸大众。汝等且观妙喜世界无动如来其国严饰。菩萨行净弟子清白。皆曰。唯然已见。佛言。若菩萨欲得如是清净佛土。当学无动如来所行之道。现此妙喜国时。娑婆世界十四那由他人发阿耨多罗三藐三菩提心。皆愿生于妙喜佛土。释迦牟尼佛即记之曰。当生彼国。时妙喜世界于此国土。所应饶益其事讫已。还复本处举众皆见。佛告舍利弗。汝见此妙喜世界及无动佛不。唯然已见。世尊。愿使一切众生得清净土如无动佛。获神通力如维摩诘。世尊。我等快得善利。得见是人亲近供养。其诸众生若今现在若佛灭后。闻此经者亦得善利。况复闻已信解受持读诵解说如法修行。若有手得是经典者。便为已得法宝之藏。若有读诵解释其义如说修行。即为诸佛之所护念。其有供养如是人者。当知即为供养于佛。其有书持此经卷者。当知其室即有如来。若闻是经能随喜者。斯人即为取一切智。若能信解此经乃至一四句偈为他说者。当知此人即是受阿耨多罗三藐三菩提记

逐句解释

维摩诘问文殊：如来以何相得见——见佛的真义

维摩诘问文殊：如来以何相得见？文殊答：如实相见——不以色身见，不以法身见，非以智见，非以觉观见。如如不动，不生不灭，不取不舍，是则见佛。这把「见佛」从一个视觉经验，转化为一种直接契入实相的体证：真正的见佛，不是看到某种形象，而是认识到实相本身。

Vimalakirti asked Manjushri: with what characteristics is the Tathagata to be seen? Manjushri answered: as reality is — not through form-body, not through dharma-body, not through wisdom, not through conceptual observation; like suchness, unmoved, not arising or ceasing, not taking or abandoning — this is seeing the Buddha

This dialogue redefines one of Buddhism's central practices — 'seeing the Buddha' (见佛). In Pure Land practice, this means visually contemplating the Buddha's form in meditation. In Zen, it can mean 'if you meet the Buddha on the road, kill him' — meaning do not mistake any form for the ultimate. Vimalakirti and Manjushri's answer points to neither: to see the Tathagata truly is to see the nature of reality itself, the 'suchness' (如如) that is always already present and never acquired.

见阿閦佛国——以神通示现妙喜世界

维摩诘对大众说：你们想见阿閦如来的妙喜世界吗？大众皆欲见。维摩诘以神力右手取妙喜世界（包括其中的须弥山、大海、阿閦佛、一切菩萨大众），置于娑婆世界，令大众得见，同时妙喜世界内的众生不觉不知，亦无增减。这是第六章「不思议解脱」在具体佛国层面的又一次示现。

Vimalakirti asked: would you like to see the Abhirati world of Akshobhya Buddha? The assembly expressed their wish; Vimalakirti reached out his right hand, took the entire Abhirati world — Mount Sumeru, the oceans, Akshobhya Buddha, all the bodhisattvas — and placed it in the Saha world for the assembly to see, while the inhabitants of Abhirati knew and felt nothing

Akshobhya ('Immovable') Buddha presides over the Abhirati ('Joyful') world in the east — one of the major Buddha-lands in Mahayana cosmology, often paired with Amitabha's western Pure Land. Vimalakirti's demonstration here is structurally identical to the mustard-seed-and-Sumeru example of Chapter 6: an entire world placed inside another without crowding or disturbance. The difference is that this is not a hypothetical example but a direct demonstration before the assembled audience.

阿閦佛现身说法，娑婆大众见之——两世界的相遇

妙喜世界置于娑婆世界之后，两土众生得以互见：娑婆大众见妙喜世界的庄严清净，妙喜世界的众生也得见娑婆世界的粗陋。阿閦如来现身，向娑婆世界大众说法，令无数众生发菩提心，证得无生法忍。维摩诘将妙喜世界还归原处，两土还复如故。这是「不思议解脱」在最大规模上的演示。

When the Abhirati world was placed within the Saha world, beings of both lands could see each other; Akshobhya Tathagata himself appeared and taught the Dharma to the Saha assembly; countless beings aroused bodhicitta and attained acceptance of non-arising; then Vimalakirti returned the Abhirati world to its place and everything was as before

The encounter between the two worlds is not merely spectacular — it is pedagogically precise. The beings of the Saha world see, perhaps for the first time, what a fully purified Buddha-land looks like. The contrast with their own impure world is immediate and vivid. Yet the sutra does not let this become an occasion for escapism or world-rejection: after the teaching, both worlds return to their proper places. The pure world does not become a permanent refuge; its glimpse is a spur to practice here.

维摩诘将生妙喜——以净行回向往生

佛陀问维摩诘：汝欲生妙喜世界否？维摩诘答：唯然世尊，愿见阿閦如来。佛陀告知大众：将来维摩诘命终，必生妙喜世界。这表明：即使在大乘菩萨道中，以功德回向净土、在净土中继续修行，也是合法且受佛陀认可的一条路。维摩诘并不排斥净土，他对净土的超越是从内心的清净出发，而非拒绝净土。

The Buddha asked Vimalakirti: would you like to be reborn in the Abhirati world? Vimalakirti said yes. The Buddha then told the assembly: when Vimalakirti's life ends, he will be born in the Abhirati world of Akshobhya Buddha

This is a quietly significant moment. Throughout the sutra, Vimalakirti has taught that the pure land is the mind itself — 'as the mind is pure, the Buddha-land is pure' (随其心净则佛土净). One might conclude from this that the external pure land is merely a conventional teaching for lesser minds. But here Vimalakirti himself expresses the wish to be reborn in Abhirati and the Buddha confirms it. The sutra holds both in tension: the ultimate teaching (pure land is the mind) and the conventional practice (aspiring to be born in a pure land) are not contradictory.

见阿閦佛品的意义——不动如如，如实见佛

本品以「如实相见」开篇，以实际示现妙喜世界作结——将「见佛」的理论（不取相、如如不动）与具体的佛国庄严（阿閦世界的实际示现）融为一体。阿閦（Akshobhya）的含义正是「不动」：如如不动，才能真正见佛；维摩诘展示妙喜世界，也是在展示「不动」的实相。全章是对「见佛」这一修行主题最完整的诠释。

The chapter opens with 'seeing the Buddha as suchness' and closes with the actual demonstration of a Buddha-land — uniting the ultimate teaching (formless, unmoved suchness) with its concrete expression (the brilliant Abhirati world of Akshobhya, whose name means 'Immovable')

The name Akshobhya — 'the Immovable One' — is the thread that holds the chapter together. Seeing the Buddha truly means seeing the unmoved suchness that is the Buddha's actual nature. Akshobhya's world is the embodiment of that quality: a world arising from the aspiration to remain unmoved by anger, unmoved by circumstances, unmoved even by the chaos and suffering of the Saha world. The chapter brings together the sutra's highest philosophical teaching (seeing without marks) and its most vivid cosmological imagery (an entire universe fitting in a hand) in a single demonstration.

总结 · Summary

见阿閦佛品以「如实相见」开篇，以整个妙喜世界置于娑婆世界的神通示现作结，将「见佛」这一修行主题的理论（不取相、如如不动）与具体的佛国庄严（阿閦世界的示现）融为一体。阿閦之名即「不动」，贯穿全章：真正的见佛是不动的直观；妙喜世界是不动之心所庄严的净土。章末佛陀确认维摩诘命终将生妙喜世界，说明心净与净土并不矛盾，而是相互印证的两面。

Chapter 12 unites the sutra's highest philosophical teaching with its most spectacular cosmological imagery. The opening question — how is the Tathagata to be seen? — receives the answer: as suchness, unmoved, without taking or abandoning any mark. This is the ultimate formless seeing. The chapter then demonstrates this principle through the most elaborate spatial miracle in the sutra: an entire Buddha-land placed within another world, with neither disturbed. Akshobhya ('the Immovable') is the perfect focal point: his name, his world, and the quality of seeing that he represents are all versions of the same insight — that what is ultimate is unshakeable by any circumstance.
