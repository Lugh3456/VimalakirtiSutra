第九章 · 入不二法门品

维摩诘所说经

← 第八章 目录 Contents 第十章 →

尔时维摩诘。谓众菩萨言。诸仁者。云何菩萨入不二法门。各随所乐说之。会中有菩萨名法自在。说言。诸仁者。生灭为二。法本不生今则无灭。得此无生法忍。是为入不二法门德守菩萨曰。我我所为二。因有我故便有我所。若无有我则无我所。是为入不二法门不眴菩萨曰。受不受为二。若法不受则不可得。以不可得故无取无舍无作无行。是为入不二法门德顶菩萨曰。垢净为二。见垢实性则无净相顺于灭相。是为入不二法门善宿菩萨曰。是动是念为二。不动则无念。无念则无分别。通达此者。是为入不二法门善眼菩萨曰。一相无相为二。若知一相即是无相。亦不取无相入于平等。是为入不二法门妙臂菩萨曰。菩萨心声闻心为二。观心相空如幻化者。无菩萨心无声闻心。是为入不二法门弗沙菩萨曰。善不善为二。若不起善不善。入无相际而通达者。是为入不二法门师子菩萨曰。罪福为二。若达罪性则与福无异。以金刚慧决了此相无缚无解者。是为入不二法门师子意菩萨曰。有漏无漏为二。若得诸法等则不起漏不漏想。不著于相亦不住无相。是为入不二法门净解菩萨曰。有为无为为二。若离一切数则心如虚空。以清净慧无所碍者。是为入不二法门那罗延菩萨曰。世间出世间为二。世间性空即是出世间。于其中不入不出不溢不散。是为入不二法门善意菩萨曰。生死涅槃为二。若见生死性则无生死。无缚无解不生不灭。如是解者。是为入不二法门现见菩萨曰。尽不尽为二。法若究竟尽若不尽皆是无尽相。无尽相即是空。空则无有尽不尽相。如是入者。是为入不二法门普守菩萨曰。我无我为二。我尚不可得非我何可得。见我实性者不复起二。是为入不二法门电天菩萨曰。明无明为二。无明实性即是明。明亦不可取离一切数。于其中平等无二者。是为入不二法门喜见菩萨曰。色色空为二。色即是空非色灭空色性自空。如是受想行识识空为二。识即是空非识灭空识性自空。于其中而通达者。是为入不二法门明相菩萨曰。四种异空种异为二。四种性即是空种性。如前际后际空故中际亦空。若能如是知诸种性者。是为入不二法门妙意菩萨曰。眼色为二。若知眼性于色不贪不恚不痴。是名寂灭。如是耳声鼻香舌味身触意法为二。若知意性于法不贪不恚不痴。是名寂灭。安住其中。是为入不二法门无尽意菩萨曰。布施回向一切智为二。布施性即是回向一切智性。如是持戒忍辱精进禅定智慧。回向一切智为二。智慧性即是回向一切智性。于其中入一相者。是为入不二法门深慧菩萨曰。是空是无相是无作为二。空即无相无相即无作。若空无相无作则无心意识。于一解脱门即是三解脱门者。是为入不二法门寂根菩萨曰。佛法众为二。佛即是法法即是众。是三宝皆无为相与虚空等。一切法亦尔。能随此行者。是为入不二法门心无碍菩萨曰。身身灭为二。身即是身灭。所以者何。见身实相者不起见身及见灭身。身与灭身无二无分别。于其中不惊不惧者。是为入不二法门上善菩萨曰。身口意善为二。是三业皆无作相。身无作相即口无作相。口无作相即意无作相。是三业无作相即一切法无作相。能如是随无作慧者。是为入不二法门福田菩萨曰。福行罪行不动行为二。三行实性即是空。空则无福行无罪行无不动行。于此三行而不起者。是为入不二法门华严菩萨曰。从我起二为二。见我实相者不起二法。若不住二法则无有识。无所识者。是为入不二法门德藏菩萨曰。有所得相为二。若无所得则无取舍。无取舍者。是为入不二法门月上菩萨曰。暗与明为二。无暗无明则无有二。所以者何。如入灭受想定无暗无明一切法相亦复如是。于其中平等入者。是为入不二法门宝印手菩萨曰。乐涅槃不乐世间为二。若不乐涅槃不厌世间则无有二。所以者何。若有缚则有解。若本无缚其谁求解。无缚无解则无乐厌。是为入不二法门珠顶王菩萨曰。正道邪道为二。住正道者则不分别是邪是正。离此二者。是为入不二法门乐实菩萨曰。实不实为二。实见者尚不见实何况非实。所以者何。非肉眼所见慧眼乃能见。而此慧眼无见无不见。是为入不二法门如是诸菩萨各各说已。问文殊师利。何等是菩萨入不二法门文殊师利曰。如我意者。于一切法无言无说。无示无识离诸问答是为入不二法门于是文殊师利。问维摩诘。我等各自说已。仁者当说。何等是菩萨入不二法门时维摩诘默然无言文殊师利叹曰。善哉善哉。乃至无有文字语言。是真入不二法门说是入不二法门品时。于此众中五千菩萨。皆入不二法门得无生法忍

逐句解释

诸仁者。各说己之入不二法门——三十二位菩萨的各自回答

维摩诘请室中三十二位菩萨各自说说「入不二法门」。每一位菩萨都给出了自己的答案——以超越某一对二元对立作为入门：生灭、垢净、善不善、罪福、有漏无漏、有为无为、我无我、明无明、色与空……每一个回答都在说：当你不再执著于任何一对对立，就进入了不二法门。

Vimalakirti invited the thirty-two bodhisattvas present to each describe their entry into the Dharma-gate of non-duality; each gave a different answer — transcending a different pair of opposites: arising and ceasing, pure and impure, good and not good, sin and merit, conditioned and unconditioned, self and no-self, ignorance and wisdom, form and emptiness

The thirty-two bodhisattvas form a remarkable chorus. Each one has found their own pair of opposites to dissolve — and each answer is correct, because non-duality is not a specific doctrine but a recognition that every pair of conceptual opposites is a conventional overlay on a reality that is neither one nor the other. The variety of the answers is itself the teaching: there is no single correct formulation of non-duality. Every fixed concept — including 'non-duality' itself — is a door that must eventually be walked through and left behind.

法自在菩萨：生灭为二——不生不灭，入不二门

法自在菩萨答：生灭为二。法本不生，今则无灭；得此无生法忍，是为入不二法门。这是三十二答中最直接的哲学陈述：一切法本来不生，故也无所谓灭；在这个「不生」的实相中，生与灭的二元对立自然消解。无生法忍正是直接通达这一实相的定力。

The bodhisattva Dharmavikurvana answered: arising and ceasing are two; all phenomena are originally non-arising, so there is no ceasing; the acceptance of this non-arising is the entry into non-duality

The acceptance of non-arising (无生法忍, anutpattika-dharma-ksanti) is one of the highest attainments described in Mahayana Buddhism. It means not just intellectually accepting that phenomena do not inherently arise, but having this recognition settle so deeply that the mind no longer flinches from it — no longer looking for a beginning or end to things, because it has seen directly that those categories were always conventional. This bodhisattva's answer is the most philosophically precise of the thirty-two.

德顶菩萨：我我所为二——无我无我所，入不二门

德顶菩萨答：我与我所为二。因有我故，便有我所；若无有我，则无我所，是为入不二法门。这是对「自我」问题最直接的解答：一切二元对立的根源，是把「我」当作一个实在的中心。「我所」（我的东西）是「我」的延伸——只要消解了对「我」的执著，「我所有」的世界也随之消解。

Dharmavikurvana answered: 'I' and 'mine' are two; because there is a self, there is 'mine'; if there is no self, there is no 'mine' — this is the entry into non-duality

This answer locates the root of all duality in the construction of a self. The entire edifice of 'my possessions, my body, my ideas, my practice, my liberation' rests on the prior assumption that there is a 'my' — a fixed, independent centre that owns and organises experience. When that assumption is seen through, the whole structure of 'mine versus not-mine' collapses. This is not nihilism — experience continues — but the compulsive sense of ownership and defensiveness that organises experience around a fixed centre quietly dissolves.

各菩萨历说二十余对——净垢、善恶、罪福、有无

三十二答的其余部分涵盖：净与不净（垢净非二）、善与不善（善恶非二）、罪与福（罪福非二）、有漏与无漏（漏无漏非二）、有为与无为（有为无为非二）、世间与出世间（世出世间非二）、生死与涅槃（生死涅槃非二）、明与无明（明无明非二）……每一对都是在某个具体的执著点上切入，说明该对立在究竟实相中并非真正的二元对立。

The remaining answers cover: pure and impure, good and not-good, sin and merit, defiled and undefiled, conditioned and unconditioned, worldly and supramundane, samsara and nirvana, ignorance and wisdom — each dissolving a different axis of opposition

Reading all thirty-two answers together, one notices that they cover the entire landscape of Buddhist conceptual maps: ethical (good/bad, sin/merit), cosmological (samsara/nirvana), epistemological (ignorance/wisdom), ontological (being/non-being, conditioned/unconditioned). The sutra is showing that non-duality is not a single insight but a thoroughgoing transformation of the entire way concepts are used. Every pair can be dissolved; no pair is the final, privileged example.

文殊师利答：言语道断——说即是二

三十二菩萨说毕，轮到文殊师利。文殊答：如我意者，于一切法无言无说，无示无识，离诸问答，是为入不二法门。这已经比所有前面的答案都更彻底：不仅超越任何一对二元对立，而且连「说出答案」这一动作本身，也是一种二元化（说者与被说之间有分别）。真正的不二，超越了一切言说。

Manjushri answered: in my view, with respect to all phenomena — no words, no speech, no pointing, no knowing, departing from all questions and answers — this is the entry into non-duality

Manjushri's answer is a significant escalation from the previous thirty-two. Every previous bodhisattva gave a verbal answer about transcending a particular pair of opposites. But the very act of giving a verbal answer already implies a speaker, a subject matter, and a listener — already a structure of duality. Manjushri sees this and refuses to give a content answer. The entry into non-duality, he says, is not itself a teaching that can be formulated without being undermined. This sets up Vimalakirti's silence perfectly.

维摩诘默然无言——雷霆之沉默

文殊说完，维摩诘一言不发。文殊赞叹：善哉善哉！乃至无有文字语言，是真入不二法门。这就是全经最著名的时刻：维摩诘的「以默答问」。沉默不是回避，不是无可奉告，而是直接示现了「言语道断、心行处灭」的实相。三十二个精彩的答案都仍然停在语言的层面；这一沉默，直接是不二。

When it was Vimalakirti's turn, he remained completely silent. Manjushri exclaimed: Excellent! Not even a single word or letter — this is the true entry into the Dharma-gate of non-duality

Vimalakirti's silence is the sutra's most celebrated moment — sometimes called the 'thunder of silence' or the 'roar of silence.' Thirty-two bodhisattvas gave excellent answers. Manjushri gave an even more refined answer about the limitations of verbal answers. Vimalakirti does not answer at all. And this non-answer is immediately recognised by Manjushri as the most perfect response — not because silence is mystically superior to speech, but because in this specific context, after this specific build-up, any verbal response would have been one step removed from the reality being pointed at. The silence is not emptiness; it is fullness that overflows every container language provides.

不二法门的意义——非一非二，超越概念

「不二」（advaya）的含义不是「一」（合并两端），也不是「无」（否定两端），而是超越二元结构本身。不是说善与恶是同一件事，而是说「善」与「恶」的边界是人为划定的，而不是宇宙固有的特征。这在实践上的意义是：解脱不是从「有执著」移动到「无执著」，而是直接看见「执著」与「不执著」的分别本身也是一种执著。

The meaning of 'non-duality' (advaya): it is not 'one' (collapsing two into one) and not 'nothing' (denying both); it is the dissolution of the binary structure itself — not claiming that good and evil are the same thing, but recognising that the boundary between them is conventionally drawn, not a fixed feature of ultimate reality

Non-duality is one of the most easily misunderstood concepts in Buddhist philosophy. It is not moral relativism (everything is equally acceptable), not monism (everything is one substance), and not nihilism (nothing exists or matters). It is a precise epistemological claim: the pairs of concepts we use to navigate reality — good/bad, pure/impure, liberated/uniliberated — are useful conventional tools, but they do not carve reality at its joints. Ultimate reality does not come pre-labelled. The sage who has realised non-duality still uses these concepts, still acts ethically, still makes distinctions in conventional contexts — but holds them lightly, knowing they are maps rather than territory.

总结 · Summary

入不二法门品是全经最著名的一章，以维摩诘的「以默答问」为顶点。三十二位菩萨各说入不二法门：每一个答案都从某一对二元对立（生灭、垢净、善恶、罪福、我与我所、生死与涅槃……）切入，说明该对立在究竟实相中的消解。文殊师利进一步指出：连说出答案本身，也仍然是一种二元化。而维摩诘的沉默，则是完全超越言说的直接示现——以沉默本身，完成了对「不二」最完整的表达。

Chapter 9 is the sutra's most celebrated chapter, built around a brilliant escalating structure. Thirty-two bodhisattvas each dissolve a different pair of conceptual opposites as their entry point into non-duality — covering the entire range of Buddhist conceptual maps, from ethics to cosmology to epistemology. Manjushri then dissolves the act of formulating an answer. Vimalakirti says nothing at all. In context, this silence is immediately recognised as the perfect response: not an absence of understanding but an overflow of it, a reality so full that no single container of language can hold it. The chapter's final lesson is that non-duality is not itself a teaching — it is the dissolving of the very framework within which teachings operate.
