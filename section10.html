第十章 · 香积佛品

维摩诘所说经

← 第九章 目录 Contents 第十一章 →

于是舍利弗心念。日时欲至。此诸菩萨当于何食。时维摩诘。知其意而语言。佛说八解脱。仁者受行。岂杂欲食而闻法乎。若欲食者且待须臾。当令汝得未曾有食。时维摩诘即入三昧。以神通力示诸大众。上方界分过四十二河沙佛土。有国名众香。佛号香积。今现在。其国香气比于十方诸佛世界人天之香最为第一。彼土无有声闻辟支佛名。唯有清净大菩萨众。佛为说法。其界一切皆以香作楼阁。经行香地苑园皆香。其食香气周流十方无量世界。时彼佛与诸菩萨方共坐食。有诸天子皆号香严。悉发阿耨多罗三藐三菩提心。供养彼佛及诸菩萨。此诸大众莫不目见。时维摩诘问众菩萨言。诸仁者。谁能致彼佛饭。以文殊师利威神力故咸皆默然。维摩诘言。仁此大众无乃可耻。文殊师利曰。如佛所言勿轻未学。于是维摩诘。不起于座居众会前化作菩萨。相好光明威德殊胜蔽于众会。而告之曰。汝往上方界。分度如四十二河沙佛土。有国名众香。佛号香积。与诸菩萨方共坐食。汝往到彼如我辞曰。维摩诘稽首世尊足下。致敬无量问讯起居少病少恼气力安不。愿得世尊所食之余。当于娑婆世界施作佛事。令此乐小法者得弘大道。亦使如来名声普闻。时化菩萨即于会前升于上方。举众皆见其去到众香界礼彼佛足。又闻其言。维摩诘稽首世尊足下。致敬无量问讯起居少病少恼气力安不。愿得世尊所食之余。欲于娑婆世界施作佛事。使此乐小法者得弘大道。亦使如来名声普闻。彼诸大士见化菩萨叹未曾有。今此上人从何所来。娑婆世界为在何许。云何名为乐小法者。即以问佛。佛告之曰。下方度如四十二河沙佛土。有世界名娑婆。佛号释迦牟尼。今现在于五浊恶世。为乐小法众生敷演道教。彼有菩萨名维摩诘。住不可思议解脱。为诸菩萨说法。故遣化来称扬我名并赞此土。令彼菩萨增益功德。彼菩萨言。其人何如乃作是化。德力无畏神足若斯。佛言。甚大。一切十方皆遣化往施作佛事饶益众生。于是香积如来。以众香钵盛满香饭与化菩萨。时彼九百万菩萨俱发声言。我欲诣娑婆世界供养释迦牟尼佛。并欲见维摩诘等诸菩萨众。佛言可往。摄汝身香。无令彼诸众生起惑著心。又当舍汝本形。勿使彼国求菩萨者而自鄙耻。又汝于彼莫怀轻贱而作碍想。所以者何。十方国土皆如虚空。又诸佛为欲化诸乐小法者。不尽现其清净土耳。时化菩萨既受钵饭。与彼九百万菩萨俱。承佛威神及维摩诘力。于彼世界忽然不现。须臾之间至维摩诘舍。时维摩诘。即化作九百万师子之座严好如前。诸菩萨皆坐其上。是化菩萨以满钵香饭与维摩诘。饭香普熏毘耶离城及三千大千世界。时毘耶离婆罗门居士等。闻是香气身意快然叹未曾有。于是长者主月盖。从八万四千人来入维摩诘舍。见其室中菩萨甚多诸师子座高广严好。皆大欢喜礼众菩萨及大弟子。却住一面。诸地神虚空神及欲色界诸天。闻此香气亦皆来入维摩诘舍。时维摩诘语舍利弗等诸大声闻。仁者可食如来甘露味饭大悲所熏无以限意食之使不消也。有异声闻念。是饭少而此大众人人当食。化菩萨曰。勿以声闻小德小智称量如来无量福慧。四海有竭此饭无尽。使一切人食揣若须弥乃至一劫犹不能尽。所以者何。无尽戒定智慧解脱解脱知见功德具足者。所食之余。终不可尽。于是钵饭悉饱众会犹故不蝾。其诸菩萨声闻天人食此饭者。身安快乐。譬如一切乐庄严国诸菩萨也。又诸毛孔皆出妙香。亦如众香国土诸树之香尔时维摩诘问众香菩萨。香积如来以何说法。彼菩萨曰。我土如来无文字说。但以众香令诸天人得入律行。菩萨各各坐香树下闻斯妙香。即获一切德藏三昧。得是三昧者。菩萨所有功德皆悉具足。彼诸菩萨问维摩诘。今世尊释迦牟尼以何说法。维摩诘言。此土众生刚强难化故。佛为说刚强之语以调伏之。言是地狱是畜生是饿鬼。是诸难处。是愚人生处。是身邪行是身邪行报。是口邪行是口邪行报。是意邪行是意邪行报。是杀生是杀生报。是不与取是不与取报。是邪婬是邪婬报。是妄语是妄语报。是两舌是两舌报。是恶口是恶口报。是无义语是无义语报。是贪嫉是贪嫉报。是瞋恼是瞋恼报。是邪见是邪见报。是悭贪是悭贪报。是毁戒是毁戒报。是瞋恚是瞋恚报。是懈怠是懈怠报。是乱意是乱意报。是愚痴是愚痴报。是结戒是持戒是犯戒。是应作是不应作。是障碍是不障碍。是得罪是离罪。是净是垢。是有漏是无漏。是邪道是正道。是有为是无为。是世间是涅槃。以难化之人心如猕猴故。以若干种法制御其心乃可调伏。譬如象马犷悷不调加诸楚毒乃至彻骨然后调伏。如是刚强难化众生故。以一切苦切之言乃可入律。彼诸菩萨闻说是已。皆曰未曾有也。如世尊释迦牟尼佛。隐其无量自在之力。乃以贫所乐法度脱众生。斯诸菩萨亦能劳谦。以无量大悲生是佛土。维摩诘言。此土菩萨于诸众生大悲坚固。诚如所言。然其一世饶益众生。多于彼国百千劫行。所以者何。此娑婆世界有十事善法。诸余净土之所无有。何等为十。以布施摄贫穷。以净戒摄毁禁。以忍辱摄瞋恚。以精进摄懈怠。以禅定摄乱意。以智慧摄愚痴。说除难法度八难者。以大乘法度乐小乘者。以诸善根济无德者。常以四摄成就众生。是为十。彼菩萨曰。菩萨成就几法。于此世界行无疮疣生于净土。维摩诘言。菩萨成就八法。于此世界行无疮疣生于净土。何等为八。饶益众生而不望报。代一切众生受诸苦恼。所作功德尽以施之。等心众生谦下无碍。于诸菩萨视之如佛。所未闻经闻之不疑。不与声闻而相违背。不嫉彼供不高己利。而于其中调伏其心。常省己过不讼彼短。以一心求诸功德。是为八法。维摩诘文殊师利。于大众中说是法时。百千天人皆发阿耨多罗三藐三菩提心。十千菩萨得无生法忍

逐句解释

众香国与香积如来——以香为佛事的净土

维摩诘以神通示现：上方世界有一佛国，名众香国，其佛号香积如来。那里没有文字说法，一切以香气传法，菩萨们以香味住于禅定和修行。维摩诘化作菩萨，前往众香国请来香饭——用来喂饱本土的大众。这个对比是全章的骨架：这个世界的「言语说法」与众香国的「香气说法」，各有其殊胜与局限。

Vimalakirti revealed that in the world above there is a Buddha-land called 'Accumulated Fragrances' whose Buddha is called Accumulated Fragrance Tathagata; there no teaching is given through words — all practice and teaching is conveyed through fragrance; Vimalakirti sent a conjured bodhisattva to request fragrant rice to feed the assembly

The 'Accumulated Fragrances' world is an imaginative counterpoint to the ordinary world of words and concepts. In that world, the Dharma is transmitted entirely through scent — a sensory channel that bypasses the verbal-conceptual faculty entirely. The fragrant rice that Vimalakirti's conjured bodhisattva brings back is imbued with the teaching of that world. When it is eaten, it will not just nourish the body but transmit something of the non-verbal Dharma of that other world. The episode is asking: what would a teaching look like that did not rely on language?

化菩萨往众香国请饭——香积如来的问答

化菩萨到众香国，香积如来问：娑婆世界的维摩诘是什么样的人？化菩萨描述了维摩诘的身份和智慧。香积如来赞叹，以大钵盛满香饭，并说：这个世界的修行者有一点特别——因为这个世界充满障碍，所以以刚强之行度化众生，这是娑婆世界菩萨的特殊功德。

The conjured bodhisattva reached the Accumulated Fragrances world; Accumulated Fragrance Buddha asked about Vimalakirti; after hearing the description, he praised the bodhisattvas of the Saha world — because this world is filled with obstacles and difficulties, the effort required to practise here generates a special merit that easier worlds do not

This exchange contains one of the sutra's quiet gems: the difficult world is the valuable world. The Saha world (our world) is full of defilement, conflict, and suffering. Beings here are 'hard to tame' — like wild horses that require extraordinary skill and effort to reach. But precisely because of this difficulty, the bodhisattvas who manage to practise and teach here develop a robustness and versatility that no easier world can produce. The fragrant world praises our messy world. This is a form of consolation and reframing: the obstacles are not obstacles to the path — they are the path.

香饭的奇迹——一钵之饭饱诸大众，犹故不尽

化菩萨带回一大钵香饭，维摩诘将其分食给所有在场的菩萨、大弟子及大众。众人皆饱，而饭尚未尽。饭香充满毘耶离城乃至三千大千世界。这是对第六章「不思议解脱」主题的再现：有限的一碗饭，容纳了无量众生的需求；气味穿透了整个宇宙的边界。一切都在说：实相不受数量的限制。

The conjured bodhisattva brought back one bowl of fragrant rice; Vimalakirti distributed it to all those present — monks, bodhisattvas, gods, and the great assembly; everyone ate their fill, yet the rice in the bowl remained inexhaustible; the fragrance filled the entire city and spread throughout the three-thousand-fold world

The inexhaustible bowl recalls the loaves and fishes of other traditions, but the sutra's point is philosophical rather than miraculous in a simple sense. The bowl is inexhaustible because, in the ultimate analysis, material reality is not governed by the arithmetic of scarcity that conventional thinking assumes. One bowl can feed thousands because the capacity of the bowl is not a fixed physical property — it is a description that applies in conventional contexts and dissolves in others. The fragrance spreading throughout the cosmos is a sensory image of the Dharma's boundless permeation.

香饭消化需持戒——不净律行者不能消此饭

大众吃饱之后，香积如来的世界来的化菩萨们感到惊讶：维摩诘如此小的室内，怎能容纳如此多的菩萨？维摩诘说明：十方诸佛以神力来此，众会各得其所，是以不觉局促。并告知：此香饭需以持清净律行方能消化——若有不净律行者，此饭不化，令其有所苦恼。这把饮食与戒律联系在一起：法食不是世间的食物，需以清净心来承受。

After the meal, Vimalakirti explained to the visiting bodhisattvas from the fragrant world: this rice requires purity of practice to digest — for those whose precepts are not pure, the rice will not be absorbed and will cause them discomfort

This teaching introduces a subtle ethical dimension to the miraculous meal. The fragrant rice is not neutral nourishment — it is imbued with the quality of its source, a world of pure practice. To receive it fully requires a corresponding purity in the receiver. This is not a gatekeeping mechanism; it is a description of how transformative teachings work. A teaching of deep non-duality will pass through a mind that is ready for it and leave little trace in one that is not — not because the teaching is withheld, but because the conditions for absorption are not yet present.

香积如来问：娑婆世界菩萨云何修行——十事善法

应化菩萨向香积如来报告维摩诘的答复：娑婆世界菩萨以十事善法庄严佛土：以大悲摄诸众生故；以无碍辩才故；以坚固志力故；以无厌足心说法故；常修善根回向菩提故；于诸佛法常生信解故；以六度化导众生故；以神通利乐众生故；以方便智慧成就一切功德故；常供养诸佛故。这十事说明：在困难的世界中修行的菩萨所需具备的特殊品质。

Vimalakirti's reply to the fragrant world was relayed: bodhisattvas in the Saha world adorn the Buddha-land through ten virtuous qualities — great compassion, eloquence without obstruction, steadfast resolve, tireless teaching, constant dedication of roots of virtue to awakening, firm faith in the Dharma, guiding beings through the six perfections, benefiting beings through spiritual powers, accomplishing all merits through wisdom and skillful means, and constant offerings to all Buddhas

The ten qualities are a compressed curriculum for the Mahayana bodhisattva in a difficult world. Each one addresses a specific challenge: tireless teaching addresses the discouragement that comes from teaching beings who are hard to reach; steadfast resolve addresses the temptation to abandon the path when obstacles multiply; great compassion addresses the risk of becoming detached from beings while practising emptiness. Together they describe a bodhisattva who is rooted, flexible, and inexhaustible — exactly what a world full of obstacles requires.

香积如来以八法教娑婆菩萨——回向与方便

香积如来也通过化菩萨向娑婆世界回传了八法：以无尽灯传授菩提心（一灯传无数灯）；布施而不求回报；以忍辱摄一切众生；以深根方便导化难化之人（如猕猴难驯，须用种种方法）；以一切善根回向无上菩提；不求小乘法；以大乘之心度化一切众生；以精进严净佛土。这八法是香积如来对娑婆世界的「回赠教法」。

Accumulated Fragrance Tathagata sent back eight teachings for the bodhisattvas of this world through the conjured bodhisattva: transmitting bodhicitta like an inexhaustible lamp; giving without expectation of return; patience with all beings; using many different methods for hard-to-teach people (like using various means to tame a wild monkey); dedicating all merit to unsurpassed awakening; not seeking the Hinayana; guiding all beings with the Mahayana mind; diligently purifying the Buddha-land

The exchange of teachings between the two worlds is the chapter's most beautiful structural feature: our world sends a conjured bodhisattva to request rice, and that world sends back teachings. The rice nourishes the body; the teachings nourish the path. The 'inexhaustible lamp' image is particularly powerful: one lamp lights another without itself becoming dimmer. The transmission of the aspiration for awakening (bodhicitta) from person to person works the same way — giving does not diminish the giver. This is the economics of wisdom, which does not obey the laws of scarcity.

总结 · Summary

香积佛品以一次跨越宇宙的「借饭」故事，展开了本经对娑婆世界修行价值的深刻反思。众香国以香气传法，不用言语；娑婆世界以言语说法，充满障碍——但正因如此，娑婆世界菩萨所具备的坚忍、方便和大悲，是其他净土中难以培养的特殊功德。一钵香饭饱诸大众、犹故不尽，是「不思议解脱」的又一次示现；香积如来以八法回赠的教导，是两个世界之间真正的法供养。全章以「以无尽灯传菩提心」作结，是本经对菩萨精神最温柔而有力的总结之一。

Chapter 10 offers an unexpected perspective: our difficult, obstacle-filled world is praised by the inhabitants of a pure, fragrant one. The exchange between the two worlds — rice for teachings — is structured as a mutual offering. The Saha world's bodhisattvas are shown to possess a rare hardiness born of practice under difficult conditions, which pure-land beings cannot cultivate in the same way. The inexhaustible bowl of rice demonstrates again the principle of the inconceivable liberation: reality is not bound by the arithmetic of scarcity. And Accumulated Fragrance Tathagata's eight returning teachings — especially the image of the inexhaustible lamp, in which one flame lights countless others without diminishing — offer some of the sutra's most enduring images of how the Dharma propagates through the world.
