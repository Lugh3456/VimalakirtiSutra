维摩诘所说经

← 第六章 目录 Contents 第八章 →

尔时文殊师利问维摩诘言。菩萨云何观于众生。维摩诘言。譬如幻师见所幻人。菩萨观众生为若此。如智者见水中月。如镜中见其面像。如热时焰。如呼声响。如空中云。如水聚沫。如水上泡。如芭蕉坚。如电久住。如第五大。如第六阴。如第七情。如十三入。如十九界。菩萨观众生为若此。如无色界色。如焦谷牙。如须陀洹身见。如阿那含入胎。如阿罗汉三毒。如得忍菩萨贪恚毁禁。如佛烦恼习。如盲者见色。如入灭尽定出入息。如空中鸟迹。如石女儿。如化人起烦恼。如梦所见已寤。如灭度者受身。如无烟之火。菩萨观众生为若此文殊师利言。若菩萨作是观者。云何行慈。维摩诘言。菩萨作是观已自念。我当为众生说如斯法。是即真实慈也。行寂灭慈无所生故。行不热慈无烦恼故。行等之慈等三世故。行无诤慈无所起故。行不二慈内外不合故行不坏慈毕竟尽故。行坚固慈心无毁故。行清净慈诸法性净故。行无边慈如虚空故。行阿罗汉慈破结贼故。行菩萨慈安众生故。行如来慈得如相故。行佛之慈觉众生故。行自然慈无因得故。行菩提慈等一味故。行无等慈断诸爱故。行大悲慈导以大乘故。行无厌慈观空无我故。行法施慈无遗惜故。行持戒慈化毁禁故。行忍辱慈护彼我故。行精进慈荷负众生故。行禅定慈不受味故。行智慧慈无不知时故。行方便慈一切示现故。行无隐慈直心清净故。行深心慈无杂行故。行无诳慈不虚假故。行安乐慈令得佛乐故。菩萨之慈为若此也文殊师利又问。何谓为悲。答曰。菩萨所作功德。皆与一切众生共之。何谓为喜。答曰。有所饶益欢喜无悔。何谓为舍。答曰。所作福祐无所悕望。文殊师利又问。生死有畏菩萨当何所依。维摩诘言。菩萨于生死畏中。当依如来功德之力。文殊师利又问。菩萨欲依如来功德之力。当于何住。答曰。菩萨欲依如来功德力者。当住度脱一切众生。又问。欲度众生当何所除。答曰。欲度众生除其烦恼。又问。欲除烦恼当何所行。答曰。当行正念。又问。云何行于正念。答曰。当行不生不灭。又问。何法不生何法不灭。答曰。不善不生善法不灭。又问。善不善孰为本。答曰身为本。又问。身孰为本。答曰。欲贪为本。又问。欲贪孰为本。答曰。虚妄分别为本。又问。虚妄分别孰为本。答曰。颠倒想为本。又问。颠倒想孰为本。答曰。无住为本。又问。无住孰为本。答曰。无住则无本。文殊师利。从无住本立一切法时维摩诘室有一天女。见诸大人闻所说法便现其身。即以天华散诸菩萨大弟子上。华至诸菩萨即皆堕落。至大弟子便著不堕。一切弟子神力去华不能令去。尔时天女问舍利弗。何故去华。答曰。此华不如法是以去之天曰。勿谓此华为不如法。所以者何。是华无所分别。仁者自生分别想耳。若于佛法出家有所分别为不如法。若无所分别是则如法。观诸菩萨华不著者已断一切分别想故。譬如人畏时非人得其便。如是弟子畏生死故。色声香味触得其便也。已离畏者一切五欲无能为也。结习未尽华著身耳。结习尽者华不著也。舍利弗言。天止此室其已久如。答曰。我止此室如耆年解脱。舍利弗言。止此久耶。天曰。耆年解脱亦何如久。舍利弗默然不答。天曰。如何耆旧大智而默。答曰。解脱者无所言说故吾于是不知所云。天曰。言说文字皆解脱相。所以者何。解脱者不内不外不在两间。文字亦不内不外不在两间。是故舍利弗。无离文字说解脱也。所以者何。一切诸法是解脱相。舍利弗言。不复以离婬怒痴为解脱乎。天曰。佛为增上慢人。说离婬怒痴为解脱耳。若无增上慢者。佛说婬怒痴性即是解脱。舍利弗言。善哉善哉。天女。汝何所得以何为证辩乃如是。天曰。我无得无证故辩如是。所以者何。若有得有证者即于佛法为增上慢舍利弗问天。汝于三乘为何志求。天曰。以声闻法化众生故我为声闻。以因缘法化众生故我为辟支佛。以大悲法化众生故我为大乘。舍利弗。如人入瞻卜林唯嗅瞻卜不嗅余香。如是若入此室。但闻佛功德之香。不乐闻声闻辟支佛功德香也。舍利弗。其有释梵四天王诸天龙鬼神等入此室者。闻斯上人讲说正法。皆乐佛功德之香发心而出。舍利弗。吾止此室十有二年。初不闻说声闻辟支佛法。但闻菩萨大慈大悲不可思议诸佛之法。舍利弗。此室常现八未曾有难得之法。何等为八。此室常以金色光照昼夜无异。不以日月所照为明。是为一未曾有难得之法。此室入者不为诸垢之所恼也。是为二未曾有难得之法。此室常有释梵四天王他方菩萨来会不绝。是为三未曾有难得之法。此室常说六波罗蜜不退转法。是为四未曾有难得之法。此室常作天人第一之乐弦出无量法化之声。是为五未曾有难得之法。此室有四大藏众宝积满。赒穷济乏求得无尽。是为六未曾有难得之法。此室释迦牟尼佛．阿弥陀佛．阿閦佛．宝德．宝炎．宝月．宝严．难胜．师子响．一切利成。如是等十方无量诸佛。是上人念时。即皆为来广说诸佛秘要法藏说已还去。是为七未曾有难得之法。此室一切诸天严饰宫殿诸佛净土皆于中现。是为八未曾有难得之法。舍利弗。此室常现八未曾有难得之法。谁有见斯不思议事。而复乐于声闻法乎。舍利弗言。汝何以不转女身。天曰。我从十二年来。求女人相了不可得。当何所转。譬如幻师化作幻女。若有人问何以不转女身。是人为正问不。舍利弗言。不也。幻无定相当何所转。天曰一切诸法亦复如是无有定相。云何乃问不转女身。即时天女以神通力。变舍利弗令如天女。天自化身如舍利弗。而问言。何以不转女身。舍利弗以天女像而答言。我今不知何转而变为女身。天曰。舍利弗。若能转此女身。则一切女人亦当能转。如舍利弗非女而现女身。一切女人亦复如是。虽现女身而非女也。是故佛说一切诸法非男非女。即时天女还摄神力。舍利弗身还复如故。天问舍利弗。女身色相今何所在。舍利弗言。女身色相无在无不在。天曰。一切诸法亦复如是。无在无不在。夫无在无不在者佛所说也。舍利弗问天。汝于此没当生何所。天曰。佛化所生吾如彼生。曰佛化所生非没生也。天曰。众生犹然无没生也。舍利弗问天。汝久如当得阿耨多罗三藐三菩提。天曰。如舍利弗还为凡夫。我乃当成阿耨多罗三藐三菩提。舍利弗言。我作凡夫无有是处。天曰。我得阿耨多罗三藐三菩提亦无是处。所以者何。菩提无住处。是故无有得者。舍利弗言。今诸佛得阿耨多罗三藐三菩提。已得当得。如河沙。皆谓何乎。天曰。皆以世俗文字数故说有三世。非谓菩提有去来今。天曰。舍利弗。汝得阿罗汉道耶。曰无所得故而得。天曰。诸佛菩萨亦复如是。无所得故而得尔时维摩诘。语舍利弗。是天女已曾供养九十二亿佛已。能游戏菩萨神通。所愿具足得无生忍住不退转。以本愿故随意能现教化众生

逐句解释

菩萨观众生若此——幻师见所幻人

文殊问维摩诘：菩萨云何观于众生？维摩诘答：如幻师见所幻人——菩萨看待众生，就像魔术师看待自己变化出来的人一样：知道那个「人」是幻现，但仍然如实与之互动。接下来是二十余个类比，每一个都指向同样的洞见：众生似真实而本无自性。

Manjushri asked: how does a bodhisattva observe sentient beings? Vimalakirti answered: like a magician seeing his own conjured people — the bodhisattva knows that beings are like illusions, yet engages with them fully and compassionately

This is the chapter's opening question and its most important answer. The magician analogy is precise: the magician knows the conjured figure is not a real independent person, yet interacts with it as if it were — because that is how the game works and because others in the audience cannot yet see through it. The bodhisattva's compassion is not diminished by seeing through the illusion; it is actually freed from the distortions that arise when we mistake the illusion for solid reality.

如水中月，如镜中面像，如热时焰——众生的二十余种比喻

维摩诘列举菩萨观众生的二十余种比喻：水中月（美丽而虚幻）、镜中像（清晰而无实体）、热时焰（炎热中的海市蜃楼）、呼声响（回声无来处）、空中云（变幻无常）、水聚沫（一触即散）、水上泡（即生即灭）、芭蕉坚（剥开无实）……每一个比喻都是对众生「有而非实」状态的不同角度的观察。

The list of similes continues: the reflection of the moon in water, a face seen in a mirror, a mirage on a hot day, an echo with no source, a cloud in the sky, foam on water, a bubble on the surface, the core of a plantain — each points to beings as apparently real but lacking inherent existence

The list is deliberately excessive — twenty-plus similes for the same insight. This rhetorical strategy works on the reader the way repeated hammer-blows work on a nail: by the time the list is finished, the idea that beings have no inherent existence has been established through so many different angles of approach that it is hard to see around it. None of the similes deny that the moon's reflection is beautiful, that the mirage appears real, that the foam is genuinely there. They only deny that these appearances have a solid, independent existence underneath them.

如无烟之火，如第五大，如第六阴——超越常规范畴的比喻

维摩诘更进一步，用不可能存在之物作比喻：如无烟之火（不可能的物质），如第五大（世间只有地水火风四大，没有第五），如第六阴（五蕴之外无第六蕴），如第七情（六根之外无第七根），如十三入（十二入之外无十三），如十九界（十八界之外无十九界）。这些比喻说明：众生就像这些「不存在之物」一样，只是约定性的名称，没有实质的所指。

Vimalakirti continues with impossible analogies: like fire without smoke, like a fifth element (there are only four), like a sixth aggregate (there are only five skandhas), like a seventh sense faculty (there are only six) — beings are like these non-existent categories: conventional names with no ultimate referent

These 'impossible' similes are philosophically sharper than the earlier ones. A cloud or a bubble at least exists conventionally. But a 'fifth great element' does not exist even conventionally — it is a label with no referent at all. By comparing sentient beings to these impossible categories, Vimalakirti is making a stronger claim: in the ultimate analysis, 'sentient being' is not just a label for something that exists without inherent nature — it is a label for something whose supposed referent cannot be found at all. This is the Madhyamaka dissolution of the category of 'person.'

行慈者，以无所生故——真实慈与寂灭慈

文殊问：若菩萨作是观，如何行慈？维摩诘答：菩萨作是观已，自念：我当为众生说如斯法。是即真实慈也。然后列举了十八种慈的类型：行寂灭慈、行不热慈、行等之慈、行无诤慈、行不二慈、行不坏慈……每一种慈都是从「无所执著」中生出来的，不是情绪性的同情，而是如实的、无条件的悲悯。

Manjushri asked: if a bodhisattva sees beings this way, how does compassion work? Vimalakirti said: precisely by seeing this way, the bodhisattva resolves to teach all beings this truth — that is true compassion. He then lists eighteen types of compassion: still compassion, non-burning compassion, equal compassion, non-contentious compassion, non-dual compassion, indestructible compassion

This is one of the sutra's most important pivots. The worry is: if you see through the illusion of 'sentient beings,' why would you bother to help them? Vimalakirti's answer is that seeing through the illusion is precisely what makes genuine compassion possible. Conventional compassion is mixed with attachment, projection, and the need to feel like a helper. Compassion rooted in the insight into emptiness is clean — it acts without the distortions that arise from mistaking the illusion for solid fact. The eighteen types of compassion are all variations on this theme: compassion that does not add to suffering by being needy, possessive, or one-sided.

室中有一天女——散花于菩萨众身，花不著

维摩诘室内有一天女，以鲜花遍撒菩萨大弟子众。花落在菩萨身上立刻落下，落在舍利弗等大弟子身上却粘附不脱。天女说：花本无分别，是你们的分别心使花粘住了你们。已断贪著者，花不能附。这个场景是本章最戏剧性的时刻，也是理论与实践之间的完美比对。

A goddess in the room scattered flowers over all the bodhisattvas and disciples; the flowers fell away from the bodhisattvas but stuck to the great disciples. The goddess said: the flowers themselves make no distinction — it is your discriminating minds that make them adhere

This is one of the sutra's most memorable images. The flower test is impartial and immediate: it shows in a single gesture who has truly let go of attachment and who has not. The irony is that the great disciples — the most senior, the most respected, the most celebrated practitioners — are the ones the flowers stick to. The bodhisattvas, practising the non-discriminating insight that has been described throughout the chapter, pass the test effortlessly. The sutra makes the gap between doctrine and realisation vivid without any need for argument.

天女与舍利弗：女身可转否——性别是空

舍利弗问天女：何不转女身？天女反问：我求女身之相了不可得，当何所转？若舍利弗舍男身，则可言转女身。天女以神通将舍利弗化为女身、自变为男身，问：今何不转女身？舍利弗以女身说：我不知何转，犹如化人。天女曰：如是，一切诸法亦复如是，无有定相。

Shariputra asked the goddess why she had not transformed her female body; she said: I have sought a female form and cannot find it — what is there to transform? She then used her power to switch bodies with Shariputra, asking him: now why don't you transform your female body? He replied: I don't know how — it's like being a conjured person. She said: just so, all phenomena have no fixed form

This episode is one of the most celebrated in all of Mahayana literature for its treatment of gender. The goddess's question cuts through Shariputra's implicit assumption that female form is a disadvantage or obstacle in practice — that being female is a thing that needs to be fixed. Her response shows that 'female form' cannot be located as a fixed, independent entity any more than any other phenomenon. When Shariputra is put in a female body and asked to change it, he discovers he has no idea how — because he, too, cannot find the 'form' that supposedly needs changing. Gender, like all phenomena, has no fixed inherent nature.

天女问法——我说法，亦无文字，说示皆平等

舍利弗问天女：在此室中久如？天女说：我处此室，如耆年解脱——无去无来，无终无始。舍利弗问：汝有何等奇特之法？天女答：我无奇特，以不异诸法。不起不灭，是为坐禅。不断诸法，是为涅槃。以无所著，遍诣诸土，是为菩萨行。最后说：我说法，亦无文字，说示皆平等。这与前面维摩诘对舍利弗的教法遥相呼应。

Shariputra asked how long the goddess had been in the room; she said: my stay here is like an elder's liberation — no coming or going, no beginning or end. She then taught: not arising and not ceasing is meditation; not severing phenomena is nirvana; going everywhere without attachment is the bodhisattva's practice; and I teach without fixed words — all teachings are equal

The goddess's final teaching reveals her as a fully realised teacher in her own right. Her definition of meditation (not arising and not ceasing), nirvana (not severing phenomena), and the bodhisattva's practice (everywhere without attachment) are precise and profound — identical in depth to what Vimalakirti has been teaching throughout. The sutra ends this chapter by showing that wisdom is not the property of any gender, rank, or role. The goddess in Vimalakirti's room has been listening to everything and has understood it all. Her teaching to Shariputra is the chapter's final word.

总结 · Summary

观众生品以「如幻师见所幻人」开场，通过二十余个比喻展示菩萨如何以无分别的眼光观察众生，然后以天女散花和舍利弗变身两个场景，将这一理论落实到具体的感知与身体上。天女的花粘在有分别心的弟子身上、却不粘在菩萨身上，是全经最生动的「理论测验」。而天女与舍利弗关于女身能否转化的对话，更是对性别执著的直接瓦解：一切诸法，无有定相，包括我们认为最固定的身份——性别，也不例外。

Chapter 7 moves from abstract philosophy to embodied demonstration. The bodhisattva's way of viewing sentient beings — as illusions, reflections, mirages — is presented through a cascade of similes, then tested and illustrated by two dramatic episodes. The flower test is the sutra's most elegant proof: discrimination makes flowers adhere; non-discrimination lets them fall. The goddess-and-Shariputra body-swap is one of Mahayana literature's most celebrated passages, using the impossibility of 'finding' a female form to demonstrate that all phenomena — including gender, which we treat as the most fixed of identities — have no inherent, locatable existence. The chapter closes with the goddess teaching Shariputra directly, embodying the sutra's central claim: wisdom has no fixed address, no required form, and no gender.
