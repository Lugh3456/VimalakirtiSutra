第二章 · 方便品

维摩諰所说经

← 第一章 目录 Contents 第三章 →

尔时毘耶离大城中有长者名维摩诘。已曾供养无量诸佛深植善本。得无生忍。辩才无碍。游戏神通逮诸总持。获无所畏降魔劳怨。入深法门善于智度。通达方便大愿成就。明了众生心之所趣。又能分别诸根利钝。久于佛道心已纯淑决定大乘。诸有所作能善思量。住佛威仪心大如海。诸佛咨嗟弟子。释梵世主所敬。欲度人故以善方便居毘耶离。资财无量摄诸贫民。奉戒清净摄诸毁禁。以忍调行摄诸恚怒。以大精进摄诸懈怠。一心禅寂摄诸乱意。以决定慧摄诸无智。虽为白衣奉持沙门清净律行。虽处居家不著三界。示有妻子常修梵行。现有眷属常乐远离。虽服宝饰而以相好严身。虽复饮食而以禅悦为味。若至博弈戏处辄以度人。受诸异道不毁正信。虽明世典常乐佛法。一切见敬为供养中最。执持正法摄诸长幼。一切治生谐偶虽获俗利不以喜悦。游诸四衢饶益众生。入治政法救护一切。入讲论处导以大乘。入诸学堂诱开童蒙。入诸婬舍示欲之过。入诸酒肆能立其志。若在长者长者中尊为说胜法。若在居士居士中尊断其贪著。若在刹利刹利中尊教以忍辱。若在婆罗门婆罗门中尊除其我慢。若在大臣大臣中尊教以正法。若在王子王子中尊示以忠孝。若在内官内官中尊化政宫女。若在庶民庶民中尊令兴福力。若在梵天梵天中尊诲以胜慧。若在帝释帝释中尊示现无常。若在护世护世中尊护诸众生。长者维摩诘。以如是等无量方便饶益众生。其以方便现身有疾。以其疾故。国王大臣长者居士婆罗门等。及诸王子并余官属。无数千人皆往问疾。其往者。维摩诘因以身疾广为说法。诸仁者。是身无常无强无力无坚。速朽之法不可信也。为苦为恼众病所集。诸仁者。如此身明智者所不怙。是身如聚沫不可撮摩。是身如泡不得久立。是身如炎从渴爱生。是身如芭蕉中无有坚。是身如幻从颠倒起。是身如梦为虚妄见。是身如影从业缘现。是身如响属诸因缘。是身如浮云须臾变灭。是身如电念念不住。是身无主为如地。是身无我为如火。是身无寿为如风。是身无人为如水。是身不实四大为家。是身为空离我我所。是身无知如草木瓦砾。是身无作风力所转。是身不净秽恶充满。是身为虚伪。虽假以澡浴衣食必归磨灭。是身为灾百一病恼。是身如丘井为老所逼。是身无定为要当死。是身如毒蛇如怨贼如空聚。阴界诸入所共合成。诸仁者。此可患厌当乐佛身。所以者何。佛身者即法身也。从无量功德智慧生。从戒定慧解脱解脱知见生。从慈悲喜舍生。从布施持戒忍辱柔和勤行精进禅定解脱三昧多闻智慧诸波罗蜜生。从方便生。从六通生。从三明生。从三十七道品生。从止观生。从十力四无所畏十八不共法生。从断一切不善法集一切善法生。从真实生。从不放逸生。从如是无量清净法生如来身。诸仁者。欲得佛身断一切众生病者。当发阿耨多罗三藐三菩提心。如是长者维摩诘。为诸问疾者如应说法。令无数千人皆发阿耨多罗三藐三菩提心

逐句解释

有长者名维摩諰。已曾供养无量诸佛深植善本

维摩諰（Vimalakirti）意为「无垃称」或「洁名」，是毞耶称中的大富长者。他曾供养无量诸佛，脚根深厚，已具足无生忍、辩才、神通等成就。本品名「方便品」，「方便」（upaya）即审机应机的善巧方法，是本章的核心主题。

There was a wealthy elder named Vimalakirti, who had made offerings to countless Buddhas and planted deep roots of virtue

Vimalakirti means "unstained reputation" or "pure name." He is a layman — not a monk — yet his attainments rival those of the greatest disciples and bodhisattvas. The chapter title 方便品 (Skillful Means) sets the theme: every apparently worldly act Vimalakirti performs is actually a method of liberation in disguise. The Sanskrit term upaya-kaushalya (skillful means) is one of Mahayana Buddhism’s most important concepts — the idea that wisdom must adapt its form to the needs of each situation and each being.

得无生忍。辩才无碍。游戏神通透诸总持。获无所畏

无生忍是通达一切法无生无灭实相而心不动摇的深度。辩才无碍是无论什么场合都能应对自如。总持（Dharani）是将一切善法持守不失的广大记忆力。这三项合起来，说明维摩諰已是一位真正的大菩萨。

He had attained the acceptance of non-arising, spoke with unobstructed eloquence, played freely with supernatural powers, and had mastered total retention (dharani)

These qualities together mark a bodhisattva of the highest level. The acceptance of non-arising (无生忍, anutpattika-dharma-ksanti) means directly realising that all phenomena neither arise nor cease — and remaining unshaken by this insight. Unobstructed eloquence means that wisdom expresses itself perfectly in any context. Dharani (总持) is the memory power that holds all teachings without losing a single one. A person with these three qualities has nothing left to learn and everything to give.

欲度人故以善方便居毞耶称

关键一句：维摩諰居于市井的目的是「欲度人故」。他没有出家隐遜，而是主动进入世间，用世界的语言和域地彘法。这一行展现了大乘菩萨道的精髓：入世间而不染于世间，方便反而成了最高的修行。

Wishing to liberate people, he used skillful means to live in Vaishali

He did not retreat to a forest monastery. He chose to live in the busiest, most cosmopolitan city in India — precisely in order to be available to everyone. This is the bodhisattva vow made concrete: instead of withdrawing from the world to protect one’s purity, one enters the world more deeply, trusting that wisdom is not contaminated by contact with suffering. Every activity described in this chapter — his business dealings, his presence in the law courts, the gambling dens and wine shops — is framed as method, not indulgence.

虽为白衣奉持沙门清净律行。虽处居家不著三界。示有妻子常修梵行。现有獷属常乐远离

本章最巧的四对矛盾：穿信徒的白衣却持净戒；居家中却不执著三界；有妻子却修梵行；有獷属却心常远离。传达的信息是：解脱并非取决于外在形式，而是心性的自由。居家也好、出家也好，关键在于内心是否执著。

Though dressed in white layman’s robes, he kept the pure discipline of a monk; though living at home, he was unattached to the three realms; though appearing to have a wife, he constantly practised celibacy; though he had a household, he always delighted in separation from it

These four paradoxes are the heart of the chapter. They overturn the assumption that liberation requires a certain external form — robes, celibacy, solitude. Vimalakirti has all of these inwardly while appearing to have none of them outwardly. The sutra is making a radical claim: the state of freedom is a quality of mind, not a dress code. A monk who clings is more "in the world" than a layman who doesn’t.

若至博弈戏处辄以度人。入诸婬舍示欲之过。入诸酒肤能立其志

这三处是方便运用的最高展现：赌场、青楼、酒店——古代社会公认的三种场所。维摩諰不回避，而是进入，并在内部完成導化。真正的自由不是静室中的隔绝，而是在污池中保持清明的能力。

When he went to gambling dens, he used the occasion to liberate people; when he entered brothels, he demonstrated the faults of desire; when he entered wine shops, he was able to establish people in right aspiration

The list builds to a crescendo. Gambling dens, brothels, wine shops — the three most condemned places in ancient Indian society — and Vimalakirti enters all of them. Not as a reformer who storms in with condemnation, but as a practitioner so free from craving that he can use each environment as a teaching. The brothel becomes a lesson on the suffering inherent in desire. The wine shop becomes a place where someone’s resolve is quietly strengthened. The sutra is saying: wisdom doesn’t need a pure environment. It brings purity with it.

若在长者长者中尊为说胜法。若在居士居士中尊断其贪著。若在刚利刚利中尊教以忍辱

维摩諰将方便运用到每一个社会阶层：对长者说胜法，对居士断贪著，对刚利说忍辱，对婆罗门断我慢，对大臣说正法，甘至梵天、帝释和护世神。对谁说什么，完全取决于对方最深的病。这就是应病与药的个性化教化。

Among elders he was the most respected and spoke the supreme Dharma; among householders he cut through their greed; among warriors he taught forbearance

The pattern repeats across every stratum of society — elders, householders, warriors, Brahmins, ministers, princes, courtiers, commoners, all the way up to Brahma, Indra, and the guardian kings of the four directions. Each gets exactly what they most need: the greedy hear about non-attachment; the arrogant hear about humility; the powerful hear about right governance; even the devas hear about impermanence. This is the full meaning of upaya — not a single fixed teaching but a living responsiveness to what each being most needs to hear.

其以方便现身有疾

全章的转折点：维摩諰生病了。但这不是真病，而是「方便现身有疾」——以疾病为善巧方便，吸引各方访客，再展开法义。「因疾说法」成为本经后文诸大章节的线索：每一大弟子和菩萨居然都拒绝前往探病。

Using skillful means, he manifested an illness in his body

The illness is not real — it is a deliberately chosen method. By falling ill, Vimalakirti creates a situation where the entire world will come to him: kings, ministers, elders, and householders all stream to his bedside. This one sentence launches the rest of the sutra: the Buddha will ask his great disciples and bodhisattvas to visit Vimalakirti — and every single one will refuse, because each remembers being utterly outmatched by him in a previous encounter. The "illness" is the most productive illness in the history of literature.

是身如聚沫不可摑摩。是身如泡不得久立。是身如炎从渴爱生。是身如詭蕉中无有坚

这四句是十一个身体比喻的开头——聚沫、水泡、火焰、詭蕉——每一个都指向同一个真理：身体看似实在，却没有任何实质的核心。渴爱（tanha）是引起识身的根本原因。詭蕉无坚心用以比喻身体没有真实的自我。

This body is like foam — you cannot grasp it; like a bubble — it cannot long endure; like a flame — born of thirst; like a plantain trunk — nothing solid inside

Vimalakirti uses his feigned illness to deliver a teaching on the body’s unreality. Foam: the body looks solid but is mostly empty space. A bubble: it appears instantly and vanishes just as fast. A flame: its very existence depends on something being consumed — in the body’s case, craving (tanha, thirst). A plantain tree: peel away every layer of the trunk and you find no solid core — a direct image of anatman, the absence of a fixed self at the centre of the body.

是身如浮云须臾变灭。是身如电念念不住。是身无主为如地。是身无我为如火

浮云一眞就消散——这是身体寿命的图像。闪电念念消逝——这是无常的图像，每一刻、每一兵都变化不停。地、火、风、水——四大元素各自没有自我，合起来也不能构成一个稳定的「我」。

This body is like a floating cloud — it vanishes in an instant; like lightning — it does not rest from moment to moment; like the earth — without a master; like fire — without a self

The similes continue through the four elements — earth, fire, wind, water — none of which has a self, and whose combination produces no self either. Lightning is the most striking image: each flash is a new event, not the continuation of a previous one. This is the Buddhist doctrine of momentariness — what we call "the body" is not a single continuous thing but a rapid succession of momentary events, each conditioned by the last. There is no "someone" inside riding along from birth to death.

佛身者即法身也。从无量功德智慧生。当发阿耗多罗三藙0三幂三菩提心

全段教法的顶点：法身（Dharmakaya）与肉身相对。肉身是四大元素和合的无常外壳；法身是从戒、定、慧、解脱知见中生的永恒实相。维摩諰的结论：想要沿疗众生之病，必须先发菩提心〔bodhicitta〕——即证得佛身、成就佛实的终极志向。

The Buddha-body is the Dharma-body itself — born from immeasurable merit and wisdom. Those who wish to attain the Buddha-body and heal all beings’ illnesses should arouse bodhicitta

The turn is dramatic. After eleven similes showing the body as foam, bubble, lightning, and illusion, Vimalakirti points to a different kind of body altogether — the Dharma-body (法身, Dharmakaya). This is not a physical thing at all. It is the living reality of enlightened wisdom itself, born from practising the six perfections, meditative stillness, and direct insight. The final prescription is simple and total: if you want to cure your illness and heal the world, arouse bodhicitta — the aspiration to become fully awakened for the sake of all beings. That is the only medicine that actually works.

总结 · Summary

方便品展示了一个全新的修行模型：完全入世的菩萨道。维摩諰体现了一个核心精神——在市井中保持清明，在贪欲中岑立志向，在世间完成出世的事业。其疾病说法将身体的无常、无我及其不可摑摸性说得极其透彻，最终指向唯一的出路：发菩提心，证得法身。

Chapter 2 presents Vimalakirti as the ideal of the fully engaged bodhisattva. Rather than withdrawing from society to protect his purity, he enters every corner of it — the market, the gambling den, the brothel, the wine shop — and uses each setting as a vehicle for liberation. His illness, too, is a method: by taking to his bed, he draws the world to him and delivers one of the sutra’s most searching teachings. The eleven similes — foam, bubble, flame, lightning, floating cloud — dismantle every illusion of the body’s solidity or permanence. The chapter closes by pointing beyond the impermanent physical body to the Dharma-body, which is not a thing at all but the living reality of awakened wisdom. The path to that body, Vimalakirti says, begins with a single act of will: the arising of bodhicitta.
