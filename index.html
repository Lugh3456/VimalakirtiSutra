Vimalakirti Sutra

维摩诘所说经

Mahayana · 大乘

The Vimalakirti Sutra is one of the most celebrated and witty texts in Mahayana Buddhism. Unlike most sutras, its central figure is not a monk but a layman — Vimalakirti, a wealthy householder of Vaishali whose wisdom surpasses every disciple and bodhisattva in the assembly. Through a series of brilliant encounters, the sutra overturns every fixed idea about practice, purity, and liberation — culminating in Vimalakirti's famous thunderous silence on the question of non-duality.
