维摩诘所说经

如是我闻。一时佛在毘耶离庵罗树园。与大比丘众八千人俱。菩萨三万二千。众所知识。大智本行皆悉成就。诸佛威神之所建立。为护法城受持正法。能师子吼名闻十方。众人不请友而安之。绍隆三宝能使不绝。降伏魔怨制诸外道。悉已清净永离盖缠。心常安住无碍解脱。念定总持辩才不断。布施持戒忍辱精进禅定智慧。及方便力无不具足。逮无所得不起法忍。已能随顺转不退轮。善解法相知众生根。盖诸大众得无所畏。功德智慧以修其心。相好严身色像第一。舍诸世间所有饰好。名称高远逾于须弥。深信坚固犹若金刚。法宝普照而雨甘露。于众言音微妙第一。深入缘起断诸邪见。有无二边无复余习。演法无畏犹师子吼。其所讲说乃如雷震。无有量已过量。集众法宝如海导师。了达诸法深妙之义。善知众生往来所趣及心所行。近无等等佛自在慧十力无畏十八不共。关闭一切诸恶趣门。而生五道以现其身。为大医王善疗众病。应病与药令得服行。无量功德皆成就。无量佛土皆严净。其见闻者无不蒙益。诸有所作亦不唐捐。如是一切功德皆悉具足

其名曰等观菩萨。不等观菩萨。等不等观菩萨。定自在王菩萨。法自在王菩萨。法相菩萨。光相菩萨。光严菩萨。大严菩萨。宝积菩萨。辩积菩萨。宝手菩萨。宝印手菩萨。常举手菩萨。常下手菩萨。常惨菩萨。喜根菩萨。喜王菩萨。辩音菩萨。虚空藏菩萨。执宝炬菩萨。宝勇菩萨。宝见菩萨。帝网菩萨。明网菩萨。无缘观菩萨。慧积菩萨。宝胜菩萨。天王菩萨。坏魔菩萨。电德菩萨。自在王菩萨。功德相严菩萨。师子吼菩萨。雷音菩萨。山相击音菩萨。香象菩萨。白香象菩萨。常精进菩萨。不休息菩萨。妙生菩萨。华严菩萨。观世音菩萨。得大势菩萨。梵网菩萨。宝杖菩萨。无胜菩萨。严土菩萨。金髻菩萨。珠髻菩萨。弥勒菩萨。文殊师利法王子菩萨。如是等三万二千人

复有万梵天王尸弃等。从余四天下来诣佛所而听法。复有万二千天帝。亦从余四天下来在会坐。并余大威力诸天．龙神．夜叉．干闼婆．阿修罗．迦楼罗．紧那罗．摩睺罗伽．等悉来会坐。诸比丘比丘尼优婆塞优婆夷俱来会坐。彼时佛与无量百千之众恭敬围绕而为说法。譬如须弥山王显于大海。安处众宝师子之座。蔽于一切诸来大众

尔时毘耶离城有长者子。名曰宝积。与五百长者子俱。持七宝盖来诣佛所。头面礼足。各以其盖共供养佛。佛之威神令诸宝盖合成一盖。遍覆三千大千世界。而此世界广长之相悉于中现。又此三千大千世界。诸须弥山雪山。目真邻陀山摩诃目真邻陀山。香山宝山金山黑山。铁围山大铁围山。大海江河川流泉源。及日月星辰。天宫龙宫。诸尊神宫。悉现于宝盖中。又十方诸佛诸佛说法亦现于宝盖中。尔时一切大众。叹佛神力叹未曾有。合掌礼佛瞻仰尊颜目不暂舍。于是长者子宝积。即于佛前以偈颂曰

目净修广如青莲　　心净已度诸禅定　　久积净业称无量　　导众以寂故稽首
既见大圣以神变　　普现十方无量土　　其中诸佛演说法　　于是一切悉见闻
法王法力超群生　　常以法财施一切　　能善分别诸法相　　于第一义而不动
已于诸法得自在　　是故稽首此法王　　说法不有亦不无　　以因缘故诸法生
无我无造无受者　　善恶之业亦不亡　　始在佛树力降魔　　得甘露灭觉道成
已无心意无受行　　而悉摧伏诸外道　　三转法轮于大千　　其轮本来常清净
天人得道此为证　　三宝于是现世间　　以斯妙法济群生　　一受不退常寂然
度老病死大医王　　当礼法海德无边　　毁誉不动如须弥　　于善不善等以慈
心行平等如虚空　　孰闻人宝不敬承　　今奉世尊此微盖　　于中现我三千界
诸天龙神所居宫　　干闼婆等及夜叉　　悉见世间诸所有　　十力哀现是化变
众皆希有皆叹佛　　今我稽首三界尊　　大圣法王众所归　　净心观佛靡不欣
各见世尊在其前　　斯则神力不共法　　佛以一音演说法　　众生随类各得解
皆谓世尊同其语　　斯则神力不共法　　佛以一音演说法　　众生各各随所解
普得受行获其利　　斯则神力不共法　　佛以一音演说法　　或有恐畏或欢喜
或生厌离或断疑　　斯则神力不共法　　稽首十力大精进　　稽首已得无所畏
稽首住于不共法　　稽首一切大导师　　稽首能断众结缚　　稽首已到于彼岸
稽首能度诸世间　　稽首永离生死道　　悉知众生来去相　　善于诸法得解脱
不著世间如莲华　　常善入于空寂行　　达诸法相无罣碍　　稽首如空无所依

尔时长者子宝积。说此偈已白佛言。世尊。是五百长者子。皆已发阿耨多罗三藐三菩提心。愿闻得佛国土清净。唯愿世尊。说诸菩萨净土之行。佛言。善哉宝积。乃能为诸菩萨问于如来净土之行。谛听谛听善思念之。当为汝说。于是宝积及五百长者子。受教而听。佛言。宝积。众生之类是菩萨佛土。所以者何。菩萨随所化众生而取佛土。随所调伏众生而取佛土。随诸众生应以何国入佛智慧而取佛土。随诸众生应以何国起菩萨根而取佛土。所以者何。菩萨取于净国。皆为饶益诸众生故。譬如有人欲于空地造立宫室随意无碍。若于虚空终不能成。菩萨如是。为成就众生故愿取佛国。愿取佛国者非于空也。宝积。当知直心是菩萨净土。菩萨成佛时不谄众生来生其国。深心是菩萨净土。菩萨成佛时具足功德众生来生其国。菩提心是菩萨净土。菩萨成佛时大乘众生来生其国。布施是菩萨净土。菩萨成佛时一切能舍众生来生其国。持戒是菩萨净土。菩萨成佛时行十善道满愿众生来生其国。忍辱是菩萨净土。菩萨成佛时三十二相庄严众生来生其国。精进是菩萨净土。菩萨成佛时勤修一切功德众生来生其国。禅定是菩萨净土。菩萨成佛时摄心不乱众生来生其国。智慧是菩萨净土。菩萨成佛时正定众生来生其国。四无量心是菩萨净土。菩萨成佛时成就慈悲喜舍众生来生其国。四摄法是菩萨净土。菩萨成佛时解脱所摄众生来生其国。方便是菩萨净土。菩萨成佛时于一切法方便无碍众生来生其国。三十七道品是菩萨净土。菩萨成佛时念处正勤神足根力觉道众生来生其国。回向心是菩萨净土。菩萨成佛时得一切具足功德国土。说除八难是菩萨净土。菩萨成佛时国土无有三恶八难。自守戒行不讥彼阙是菩萨净土。菩萨成佛时国土无有犯禁之名。十善是菩萨净土。菩萨成佛时命不中夭。大富梵行所言诚谛。常以软语眷属不离。善和诤讼言必饶益。不嫉不恚正见众生来生其国。如是宝积。菩萨随其直心则能发行。随其发行则得深心。随其深心。则意调伏。随意调伏则如说行。随如说行则能回向。随其回向则有方便。随其方便则成就众生。随成就众生则佛土净。随佛土净则说法净。随说法净则智慧净。随智慧净则其心净。随其心净则一切功德净。是故宝积。若菩萨欲得净土当净其心。随其心净则佛土净

尔时舍利弗。承佛威神作是念。若菩萨心净则佛土净者。我世尊本为菩萨时意岂不净。而是佛土不净若此。佛知其念即告之言。于意云何。日月岂不净耶。而盲者不见。对曰不也。世尊。是盲者过非日月咎。舍利弗。众生罪故不见如来佛土严净。非如来咎。舍利弗。我此土净而汝不见。尔时螺髻梵王语舍利弗。勿作是意。谓此佛土以为不净。所以者何。我见释迦牟尼佛土清净。譬如自在天宫。舍利弗言。我见此土。丘陵坑坎荆蕀沙砾。土石诸山秽恶充满。螺髻梵言。仁者心有高下。不依佛慧故。见此土为不净耳。舍利弗。菩萨于一切众生。悉皆平等。深心清净。依佛智慧则能见此佛土清净。于是佛以足指按地。即时三千大千世界若干百千珍宝严饰。譬如宝庄严佛无量功德宝庄严土。一切大众叹未曾有。而皆自见坐宝莲华。佛告舍利弗。汝且观是佛土严净。舍利弗言。唯然世尊。本所不见。本所不闻。今佛国土严净悉现。佛语舍利弗。我佛国土常净若此。为欲度斯下劣人故。示是众恶不净土耳。譬如诸天共宝器食随其福德饭色有异。如是舍利弗。若人心净便见此土功德庄严。当佛现此国土严净之时。宝积所将五百长者子皆得无生法忍。八万四千人皆发阿耨多罗三藐三菩提心。佛摄神足。于是世界还复如故。求声闻乘三万二千天及人。知有为法皆悉无常。远尘离垢得法眼净。八千比丘不受诸法漏尽意解

逐句解释

如是我闻。一时佛在毘耶离庵罗树园

「如是我闻」是佛经的传统开场，意为「我是这样亲耳听到的」，出自阿难尊者之口。场景设在毘耶离（古印度吠舍离城），地点是庵罗树园——一片芒果林，由施主菴罗婆梨所捐。毘耶离是当时印度最自由开放的共和制城邦，以商业繁荣和多元风气著称，与本经「在家修行」的主题遥相呼应。

Thus have I heard: at one time the Buddha was staying in the Amra Grove near Vaishali

The sutra opens with Ananda's authenticating phrase "Thus have I heard." Vaishali (毘耶离) was one of ancient India's most important cities — a prosperous republic known for its cosmopolitan, open culture, governed by a council rather than a king. The Amra Grove (庵罗树园, literally "mango grove") was donated by a courtesan named Amrapali. The choice of this location over the more typical Shravasti is deliberate: Vaishali was a city of lay life, commerce, and freedom — the perfect setting for a sutra that will argue, again and again, that wisdom belongs to the layperson as much as the monk.

与大比丘众八千人俱。菩萨三万二千

佛陀身边有八千位比丘和三万二千位菩萨——菩萨人数是比丘的四倍，这是大乘经典的典型信号：本经的主角是菩萨道，而非声闻道。

Together with 8,000 great monks and 32,000 bodhisattvas

The ratio is the sutra's first signal: bodhisattvas outnumber monks four to one. In texts centred on the Theravada path, the ordained sangha is central. Here the Mahayana shift is announced from the opening line. Which makes it all the more striking that Vimalakirti — a layman — will later leave all of them speechless.

大智本行皆悉成就……为大医王善疗众病，应病与药令得服行……如是一切功德皆悉具足

这段长篇描述了三万二千菩萨的共同德行：大智本行皆已成就，诸佛威神所建立；为护正法能师子吼；念定总持辩才不断；六波罗蜜无不具足；如大医王，应病与药；无量功德皆成就，无量佛土皆庄严。这不是修辞堆砌，而是一份浓缩的菩萨道行指南——列出了一位成就者应当具备的一切内在品质。

All their great wisdom and foundational practices fully accomplished… like great physician-kings, they heal all illnesses, prescribing the right medicine for each patient… all such virtues complete

This long description of the bodhisattvas' qualities is itself a teaching. It lists the full spectrum of what a mature bodhisattva has developed: unobstructed liberation always present; mindfulness, concentration, and total recall (念定总持) unbroken; all six perfections mastered; the ability to read the specific needs of each being and offer exactly what that being needs — like a great physician who does not give the same medicine to every patient but diagnoses first and prescribes precisely. The closing phrase "all such virtues fully complete" signals that these are not aspirants but fully accomplished masters. The assembly is not a gathering of students — it is a gathering of peers. Which makes what follows even more remarkable.

其名曰等观菩萨……观世音菩萨。得大势菩萨……弥勒菩萨。文殊师利法王子菩萨。如是等三万二千人

经文随后列出了在场菩萨的名号，从等观、不等观、等不等观等较少人知的名号，到耳熟能详的观世音菩萨（慈悲的化身）、弥勒菩萨（未来佛）、文殊师利菩萨（智慧的化身）。列名是佛经的传统，既是礼敬，也是对听众说：这些都是真实存在的修行典范，不是抽象符号。文殊师利排在最后，是后文的重要伏笔——他将是唯一有资格探望维摩诘的人。

Their names: Bodhisattva Samadarśin… Avalokiteśvara, Mahāsthāmaprāpta… Maitreya, Mañjuśrī the Dharma Prince — such were the 32,000

The roll-call of bodhisattvas moves from lesser-known figures to the most celebrated in the Mahayana pantheon. Avalokiteśvara (观世音) embodies compassion; Mahāsthāmaprāpta (得大势) embodies the power of the Buddha's vow; Maitreya (弥勒) is the future Buddha, presently a bodhisattva in Tushita Heaven. Last named is Mañjuśrī (文殊师利), the bodhisattva of wisdom — and his position at the end of the list is a narrative signal. He will be the only figure in the sutra whose wisdom is great enough to engage Vimalakirti on equal terms. All others will decline to visit the sick layman. Mañjuśrī alone will go — and will return humbled and transformed.

复有万梵天王……诸比丘比丘尼优婆塞优婆夷俱来会坐。譬如须弥山王显于大海，安处众宝师子之座，蔽于一切诸来大众

除菩萨外，还有万位梵天王、万二千天帝，以及龙神、夜叉、干闼婆、阿修罗、迦楼罗、紧那罗、摩睺罗伽等八部众，以及比丘、比丘尼、优婆塞、优婆夷（在家男女众）。出席者囊括了佛教宇宙论中的一切存在层次——天、人、鬼、神、修罗。最后以须弥山矗立大海为喻，描述佛陀端坐狮子座，威严地蔽覆一切与会大众。

Also present: ten thousand Brahma kings, twelve thousand Indras, dragons, yakshas, gandharvas, asuras, garudas, kinnaras, mahoragas, monks, nuns, laymen and laywomen — all gathered. Like Mount Sumeru rising from the great ocean, the Buddha was seated on a jewelled lion throne, towering over all who had come

The assembly is cosmic in scope. It includes every category of being in the Buddhist universe: the heavenly (Brahma kings and Indras), the semi-divine (the eight classes of spirits — dragons, yakshas, gandharvas, asuras, garudas, kinnaras, mahoragas), and all four orders of the human sangha (monks, nuns, laymen, laywomen). The Mount Sumeru simile captures both the Buddha's presence and the relationship between teacher and assembly: as the mountain rises from the ocean without effort, completely natural in its supremacy, so the Buddha sits — not dominating the assembly through authority, but simply being what he is, so that all else arranges itself around him.

佛之威神令诸宝盖合成一盖，遍覆三千大千世界……诸须弥山、雪山……大海江河川流泉源，及日月星辰，天宫龙宫，诸尊神宫，悉现于宝盖中。又十方诸佛说法亦现于宝盖中

五百把七宝华盖在佛的神力下合为一盖，覆盖整个三千大千世界。盖中映现了宇宙的一切：须弥山、雪山、目真鄰陀山等诸山；铁围山、大铁围山；大海江河泉源；日月星辰；天宫、龙宫、诸神宫殿；乃至十方诸佛正在说法的场景。这是华严式的「一中含多、多即是一」的视觉示现，也是本章净土教义的第一个象征：一切清净就在当下，就在这顶合一的宝盖之中。

All the mountains — Sumeru, the Himalayas, Mucilinda and Mahā-Mucilinda… the iron mountain ranges, the great ocean, rivers, springs, the sun, moon and stars, the palaces of heaven, of dragons, of the gods — all appeared within the canopy. And the Buddhas of the ten directions, each teaching the Dharma, also appeared within the canopy

The canopy is not simply large — it is all-containing. Everything in the cosmos appears within it: the great mountain ranges of Buddhist cosmology, the oceans and rivers, the celestial bodies, the heavenly realms, and — most remarkably — the Buddhas of all ten directions simultaneously teaching the Dharma. This last detail is crucial: it means that within this single merged object, the entire activity of liberation across the whole universe is visible at once. The canopy becomes a model of Mahayana reality: one vessel, all worlds; one mind, all teachings. It is the same insight that Indra's net images in the Avatamsaka tradition — each jewel reflects all others, and the whole is present in every part.

目净修广如青莲，心净已度诸禅定，久积净业称无量，导众以寂故稽首

这是宝积赞偈十八颂的第一颂。四句分别赞叹佛的眼净如青莲（清净与慈悲的象征）、心已超越一切禅定境界、积累了无量清净业行、以寂静引导众生。青莲是梵语「优钵罗」的意译，是佛眼最常用的比喻：出淤泥而不染，宽广而清澈。

Your eyes, pure and broad as blue lotuses / Your mind, already beyond all meditative states / Long have you accumulated immeasurable pure karma / You lead beings with stillness — I bow to you

Ratnakara's praise opens with the Buddha's eyes — a sensory, intimate beginning. The blue lotus (青莲, Sanskrit utpala) is the classic image for the Buddha's eyes in Indian poetry: wide, pure, unstained, as the lotus rises from mud without being muddied. The second line is more surprising: the Buddha's mind has already "passed through" all meditative states — meaning he is not still in meditation, still climbing, still seeking. He has completed the path. The third line honours the immeasurable accumulation of practice across lifetimes. The fourth sets the keynote for the whole sutra: the Buddha leads not by force or command but by stillness — by the quality of his own being, which acts as an orientation point for others.

无我无造无受者，已无心意无受行……善恶之业亦不亡，而悉摧伏诸外道……说法不有亦不无，以因缘故诸法生，于第一义而不动

这几颂触及了佛法最深的义理：无我（没有一个固定的「我」在造业或受报）、无心意受行（超越意识活动的执取）、善恶业不失而诸外道皆被摧伏；说法时不落「有」边也不落「无」边，以缘起说明诸法的生起，而于第一义（胜义谛、空性）中如如不动。这是对中道的赞颂：既不断灭，也不常住；既非虚无，也非实有。

No self, no creator, no recipient of results / Beyond mental activity and its grasping… good and bad karma do not perish, yet all non-Buddhist views are overcome… teaching that things neither exist nor do not exist, that all phenomena arise through conditions, unmoving in ultimate truth

These stanzas touch the philosophical core of the sutra. The "three marks" implied here — no self, no fixed agent, no permanent recipient — dissolve the ordinary sense of a person who does things and collects results. The line "good and bad karma do not perish" is crucial: the teaching of no-self does not mean that actions have no consequences. It means that consequences unfold without a fixed self behind them — like a flame that burns without a "burner." The teaching of neither existence nor non-existence (不有亦不无) is the classical formulation of the Middle Way: reality is not a void (nihilism) nor a fixed entity (eternalism) but a dynamic process of dependent arising. "Unmoving in ultimate truth" (于第一义而不动) means the Buddha speaks from a place that is not disturbed by the ordinary pull of concepts — including the concept of emptiness.

始在佛树力降魔，三转法轮于大千，其轮本来常清净，天人得道此为证。度老病死大医王，以斯妙法济群生，一受不退常寂然

这几颂赞叹佛陀成道与转法轮的历史时刻：在菩提树下降伏魔罗，然后三转法轮（示转、劝转、证转）于三千大千世界。法轮本来清净——意指这不是人为创造的法，而是对实相的如实揭示。天人由此得道。佛如大医王度越生老病死，以妙法济度众生；一旦接受这法，便不退转，常住寂然。

First, beneath the Bodhi tree, he overcame Mara by his own strength / Then three times he turned the Dharma wheel across the three-thousand-fold world / The wheel has always been pure from the beginning / Gods and humans attained the path — this is the proof / Great physician-king who heals old age, sickness and death / With this wondrous Dharma he saves all beings / Once received, one never regresses — always dwelling in stillness

These stanzas compress the entire narrative arc of a Buddha's life into a handful of lines. The defeat of Mara (魔罗) at the Bodhi tree represents the conquest of the three poisons — greed, hatred, and delusion — in their most concentrated form. The three turnings of the wheel (三转法轮) refer to the three ways of teaching the Four Noble Truths: demonstrating them directly, urging the listener to apply them, and attesting that the teacher has fully realised them. The phrase "the wheel has always been pure from the beginning" (其轮本来常清净) is a profound aside: the Dharma the Buddha teaches is not something invented; it is the nature of reality itself, always already present, now revealed. The non-regression (不退) promised to those who receive this teaching echoes the stage of stream-entry — the first irreversible step on the path.

三宝于是现世间，当礼法海德无边……今我稽首三界尊……稽首一切大导师，稽首永离生死道，稽首已得无所畏，稽首已到于彼岸，稽首如空无所依

最后几颂是宝积对三宝（佛、法、僧）的礼敬，以及对佛的一系列顶礼：稽首三界之尊、一切大导师、已永离生死道者、已得无所畏者、已到彼岸者、如空无所依者。「如空无所依」是赞偈的终点，也是全偈的最高处：佛如虚空，无处不在，却无所倚附——这是对空性最诗意的表达。

The Three Jewels now appear in the world — bow to the boundless ocean of virtue of the Dharma… I now bow to the Honoured One of the Three Realms… homage to the great guide of all, homage to one who has forever left the path of birth and death, homage to one who has attained fearlessness, homage to one who has reached the other shore, homage to one who is like space, resting on nothing

The closing homages of Ratnakara's verses build to a sustained crescendo. Each "稽首" (I bow) adds another facet of what the Buddha has accomplished and become. "Forever leaving the path of birth and death" (永离生死道) does not mean the Buddha has disappeared from the world — it means he moves through it without being caught by its cycles. "Fearlessness" (无所畏) refers to the four kinds of fearlessness specific to a Buddha: certainty in his own awakening, certainty in his teaching of liberation, certainty in identifying obstacles, and certainty in the path out of suffering. The final image — "like space, resting on nothing" (如空无所依) — is the verse's most beautiful line, and one of the most quoted in Chinese Buddhist poetry. Space does not lean on anything; it encompasses everything without being held by anything. This is the Buddha's condition: fully present, completely free.

众生之类，是菩萨佛土

这是佛陀回答「净土之行」问题的核心：菩萨的佛土，就是众生本身。净土不在遥远的他方，而就在菩萨所化度的众生之中。这句话彻底颠覆了「净土在彼处、秽土在此处」的二元对立，是全章乃至全经的枢纽命题。

Sentient beings are the bodhisattva's Buddha land

Six words that overturn the ordinary understanding of the pure land. The expected answer to "how does a bodhisattva purify the Buddha land?" is: through merit, ritual, renunciation of this defiled world. The Buddha says the opposite. The bodhisattva's pure land is not a refuge from sentient beings — it is constituted by them. Wherever there are beings to help, that is the field in which the pure land is built. The sutra refuses the exit from the world of suffering; it insists instead on a transformation within it.

直心是菩萨净土……深心是菩萨净土……菩提心是菩萨净土……布施、持戒、忍辱、精进、禅定、智慧是菩萨净土……四无量心、四摄法、方便、三十七道品、回向心……十善是菩萨净土

佛陀列举了净土的内在来源，从三种心（直心、深心、菩提心）到六波罗蜜（布施、持戒、忍辱、精进、禅定、智慧）、四无量心（慈悲喜舍）、四摄法、方便、三十七道品、回向心，乃至十善道。每一种修行都是一片净土的种子：菩萨成就哪种修行，便吸引与之相应的众生共住其国。净土不是外在工程，而是内在品质的外化。

A sincere mind is the bodhisattva's pure land… a profound mind… bodhicitta… generosity, precepts, patience, effort, meditation, wisdom… the four immeasurables, the four means of attraction, skillful means, the thirty-seven factors of awakening, dedication of merit… the ten virtues — each is the bodhisattva's pure land

The Buddha's answer unfolds as a comprehensive map of the Mahayana path — and names every element of that path as a Buddha land. The "sincere mind" (直心) is a mind without flattery or self-deception. The "profound mind" (深心) is a settled, rooted aspiration. Bodhicitta (菩提心) is the great Mahayana vow to attain Buddhahood for the liberation of all beings. Then follow the six perfections, the four immeasurables (loving-kindness, compassion, sympathetic joy, equanimity), the four means of attraction (generosity, kind speech, beneficial action, cooperation), the thirty-seven factors of awakening, and the ten virtues. For each: when the bodhisattva attains Buddhahood, the beings who share that quality naturally arise in that land. The pure land is not a location — it is a resonance. A world takes the shape of the minds that inhabit it.

随其直心则能发行。随其发行则得深心。随其深心则意调伏。随意调伏则如说行……随成就众生则佛土净。随佛土净则说法净。随说法净则智慧净。随智慧净则其心净。随其心净则一切功德净

佛陀随后揭示了一条完整的因果链：直心→发起修行→深心→意志调伏→如说而行→能回向→有方便→成就众生→佛土净→说法净→智慧净→心净→一切功德净。这不是线性的「先做A再做B」，而是一个相互增益、螺旋上升的圆融过程：每一步都为下一步创造条件，最终又回归到「心净」，形成自我强化的修行循环。

A sincere mind leads to the arising of practice. Practice leads to a profound mind. A profound mind leads to mental discipline. Discipline leads to action in accord with the teaching… benefiting beings leads to a pure Buddha land. A pure Buddha land leads to pure teaching. Pure teaching leads to pure wisdom. Pure wisdom leads to a pure mind. A pure mind leads to all virtues being pure

This causal chain is one of the most elegant passages in the chapter. What looks at first like a simple linear sequence — do A, get B — turns out to be a circle. The chain begins with sincerity of mind and ends with purity of mind. But the purity of mind at the end is not the same as the sincerity at the beginning — it has been deepened, tested, and refined through the entire circuit of practice. Each stage enables the next: you cannot have skillful means without having first disciplined the mind; you cannot purify the Buddha land without first benefiting individual beings; you cannot have pure wisdom without a pure teaching to sustain it. The point is that no element is separable from the rest. You cannot shortcut to "pure mind" without the whole process — and the whole process is already implicit in the first step of sincerity.

随其心净，则佛土净

这是全章最核心的一句话，也是全经的基石。净土不在他方，就在心中。这不是说「主观上觉得净就够了」，而是说：内心的真实转化，会真实改变世界的呈现方式。是故，若菩萨欲得净土，当净其心——随其心净，则佛土净。

As the mind is pure, so is the Buddha land pure

Seven characters that contain the entire teaching of the chapter. This is not a statement about subjective perception ("if I think it's pure, it's pure"). It is a statement about the nature of reality: mind and world are not two separate things, with the world "out there" waiting to be improved while the mind watches from inside. The quality of a world arises from the quality of the minds inhabiting it. A Buddha land is pure not because it has been scrubbed clean of difficulties, but because it is seen, inhabited, and co-created by minds that have been freed from greed, hatred, and delusion. This line became foundational in East Asian Buddhism — quoted by Chan masters, Tiantai philosophers, and Pure Land teachers alike, each finding in it a basis for their own understanding of practice and liberation.

若菩萨心净则佛土净者，我世尊本为菩萨时意岂不净？而是佛土不净若此

舍利弗心中暗想：如果心净则佛土净，那世尊当年修菩萨道时，心难道不净吗？为何我们这个世界如此秽恶？这是一个诚实而尖锐的问题——它代表所有曾经怀疑「净土在心中」这一说法的人。佛陀知道他的心念，随即回应。

If the Buddha land is pure when the mind is pure, then when our World-Honoured One practised as a bodhisattva, was his mind not pure? Why then is this world so impure?

Shariputra (舍利弗), foremost of the Buddha's disciples in wisdom, voices the obvious objection — silently, in his mind, not aloud. The sutra specifies that the Buddha reads his thought, which itself is a teaching: the Buddha's awareness is not limited to what is said. Shariputra's question is genuine and important. It would be easy to dismiss "the mind creates the world" as mere idealism — a comfortable teaching that ignores the reality of suffering. Shariputra refuses that comfort. His challenge speaks for every person who has heard "change your mind, change your world" and wanted to ask: but what about the actual suffering of actual people in this actual broken world?

是盲者过，非日月咎……仁者心有高下，不依佛慧故，见此土为不净耳

佛陀以日月作喻：日月本自光净，盲者不见，不是日月的过失。众生因业障见不到佛土的庄严，不是佛土本身不净。随后螺髻梵王对舍利弗直言：我看到释迦牟尼的佛土，清净如自在天宫。舍利弗说他看到丘陵坑坎、荆蕀沙砾。螺髻梵王回应：这是因为仁者「心有高下」——尚有分别执取，未依佛慧，所以见此土为不净。

A blind person cannot see the sun and moon — that is the blind person's limitation, not the fault of the sun and moon… Your mind has distinctions of high and low; because you do not rely on the Buddha's wisdom, you see this land as impure

The Buddha's response does not deny the reality of what Shariputra sees. It relocates the source of the impurity. The blind man analogy is precise: the sun is fully present, fully bright; the limitation is entirely in the perceiver. Then the Brahma king Sikhi (螺髻梵王, "topknot Brahma") adds a sharper point: I see this very land as pure as a celestial palace. The contrast is vivid — Shariputra sees gullies and thorns; Sikhi sees a pure land. Same world, different perception. Sikhi identifies the cause: 心有高下 — "your mind has high and low," meaning it still makes discriminations, still ranks and judges, still operates with a self that separates the pure from the impure. The Buddha's wisdom (佛慧) sees without this filter — and what it sees is the world as it actually is, beneath the overlay of conditioned perception.

于是佛以足指按地，即时三千大千世界若干百千珍宝严饰……一切大众叹未曾有，而皆自见坐宝莲华

佛陀以足趾轻按大地，整个三千大千世界立刻变成无数珍宝庄严的净土，犹如宝庄严佛的无量功德宝庄严国土。一切大众惊叹从未见过如此景象，且各自发现自己坐在宝莲华上。佛陀说：我的佛土常净若此，只是为度化根器低劣的众生，才示现这片秽土。如天人共宝器食，随福德不同饭色各异——这个世界之所以显现为不净，是因为观者的福德与智慧各有差异。佛收神足，世界还复如故。

The Buddha pressed his toe upon the ground, and instantly the three-thousand-fold world system was adorned with hundreds of thousands of jewels, like the infinitely adorned land of the Buddha Ratnāvyūha… the entire assembly was awestruck, each finding themselves seated on a jewelled lotus flower

This is the chapter's climactic demonstration. With a single effortless gesture — a toe pressed to the ground — the Buddha reveals what this world actually is beneath the veil of conditioned perception. The transformation is total, immediate, and inclusive: every being present finds themselves seated on a jewelled lotus. Then the Buddha explains the vision: this is how my land always is. The "impure" world is not the fundamental reality — it is a display adjusted to the limited perceptions of beings who need to encounter suffering in order to be moved toward liberation. The analogy of the shared jewelled bowl (诸天共宝器食) is illuminating: the same food appears as different colours to beings of different merit. The world is like that bowl — one reality, perceived differently according to the capacity of the perceiver. When the Buddha lifts his foot, the vision ends. The world reverts. Both are real; neither is final.

宝积所将五百长者子皆得无生法忍。八万四千人皆发阿耨多罗三藐三菩提心……三万二千天及人得法眼净。八千比丘不受诸法漏尽意解

章末记录了法会的果报，体现了「佛以一音演说法，众生随类各得解」的活生生示现：五百长者子证得无生法忍（体证诸法不生不灭的深层实相）；八万四千人发起菩提心；三万二千天人证得法眼净（初见实相的慧眼）；八千比丘漏尽意解，断尽一切烦恼而获解脱。同一场法会，四种结果，对应四种根器。

The 500 young men with Ratnakara all attained patient acceptance of the unborn nature of all things; 84,000 people aroused the mind of awakening; 32,000 gods and humans attained the pure dharma eye; 8,000 monks, no longer grasping at any phenomenon, were fully liberated

The chapter closes with four concentric circles of realisation — a living demonstration of the sutra's own teaching. The innermost circle: the 500 young laymen who asked the question attain the deepest realisation — 无生法忍, the patient acceptance of the unborn nature of phenomena, which means resting in the recognition that nothing ever truly arises or ceases, that all apparent arising is empty of inherent existence. The second circle: 84,000 people arouse bodhicitta, the great Mahayana vow. The third: 32,000 attain 法眼净, the "pure dharma eye" — the insight of stream-entry, the first irreversible step on the path. The fourth: 8,000 monks reach full liberation through the exhaustion of all taints. Four groups, four levels, one teaching, one moment. It is the proof, in action, of Ratnakara's verse: "The Buddha teaches with a single sound; beings of all kinds each understand according to their own nature."

总结 · Summary

第一章以毘耶离的盛大法会开场。宝积率五百人献七宝盖，佛以神力合为一盖、映现三千世界——宇宙间的山河日月、天宫神宫、乃至十方诸佛说法，全都现于盖中。宝积以长偈赞佛（十八颂），赞叹佛的清净眼、超越禅定的心、降魔成道、三转法轮、以一音说法令众生各得所解，最后以「如空无所依」作结。随后宝积发问：菩萨如何净化佛土？佛陀的回答颠覆了惯常想象：净土就在众生之中，建立于直心、深心、菩提心，以及布施、持戒、忍辱等一切修行之上。佛随后揭示了一条因果圆融之链，以「随其心净则佛土净」作结。舍利弗暗生疑惑，引出盲人见日月之喻与螺髻梵王的点拨。最后佛陀以足指按地，世界刹那化为庄严净土，大众各见自坐宝莲华——对「心净则土净」的活生生示现。佛收神足，世界复原，与会大众各得与己相应的证果。

Chapter 1 opens with a cosmic assembly in Vaishali's Amra Grove — 8,000 monks, 32,000 bodhisattvas (including Avalokiteśvara, Maitreya, and Mañjuśrī), ten thousand Brahma kings, twelve thousand Indras, and all eight classes of spirits. The young layman Ratnakara leads 500 companions in offering jewelled parasols; the Buddha merges them into a single canopy reflecting every mountain, ocean, sun, moon, heavenly palace, and the teaching Buddhas of all ten directions — a first demonstration that one contains all. Ratnakara then offers eighteen stanzas of praise, moving from the Buddha's lotus-pure eyes and mind beyond meditation, through the conquest of Mara and the turning of the Dharma wheel, to the miraculous one-voice teaching that each being hears according to their own nature, ending with the image of the Buddha as space — resting on nothing, encompassing all. Ratnakara then asks: how does a bodhisattva purify a Buddha land? The Buddha's answer overturns every expectation: sentient beings themselves are the bodhisattva's Buddha land. It is built not of external material but of interior qualities — sincerity, profound aspiration, bodhicitta, and the full arc of the path from the six perfections through the thirty-seven factors of awakening. These qualities form a causal chain, each enabling the next, culminating in the sutra's central teaching: as the mind is pure, so is the Buddha land pure. Shariputra's silent objection — if the mind is pure, why is this world impure? — draws the blind man and sun analogy and Brahma Sikhi's pointed reply: your mind still makes distinctions; it does not yet rely on the Buddha's wisdom. Then the Buddha presses his toe to the ground, and the world transforms into a resplendent jewelled land — every being seated on a lotus — before reverting to its ordinary appearance. The chapter closes with four groups attaining four different realisations from the same teaching: the living proof that the Dharma truly meets each being exactly where they are.
