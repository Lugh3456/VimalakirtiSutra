维摩诘所说经

尔时文殊师利问维摩诘言。菩萨云何通达佛道。维摩诘言。若菩萨行于非道。是为通达佛道。又问。云何菩萨行于非道。答曰。若菩萨行五无间而无恼恚。至于地狱无诸罪垢。至于畜生无有无明憍慢等过。至于饿鬼而具足功德。行色无色界道不以为胜。示行贪欲离诸染著。示行瞋恚于诸众生无有恚阂。示行愚痴而以智慧调伏其心。示行悭贪而舍内外所有不惜身命。示行毁禁而安住净戒。乃至小罪犹怀大惧。示行瞋恚而常慈忍。示行懈怠而懃修功德。示行乱意而常念定。示行愚痴而通达世间出世间慧。示行谄伪而善方便随诸经义。示行憍慢而于众生犹如桥梁。示行诸烦恼而心常清净。示入于魔而顺佛智慧不随他教。示入声闻而为众生说未闻法。示入辟支佛而成就大悲教化众生。示入贫穷而有宝手功德无尽。示入刑残而具诸相好以自庄严。示入下贱而生佛种姓中具诸功德。示入羸劣丑陋而得那罗延身。一切众生之所乐见。示入老病而永断病根超越死畏。示有资生而观无常实无所贪。示有妻妾采女而常远离五欲淤泥。现于讷钝而成就辩才总持无失。示入邪济而以正济度诸众生。现遍入诸道而断其因缘。现于涅槃而不断生死。文殊师利。菩萨能如是行于非道。是为通达佛道于是维摩诘问文殊师利。何等为如来种。文殊师利言。有身为种。无明有爱为种。贪恚痴为种。四颠倒为种。五盖为种。六入为种。七识处为种。八邪法为种。九恼处为种。十不善道为种。以要言之。六十二见及一切烦恼皆是佛种。曰何谓也。答曰。若见无为入正位者。不能复发阿耨多罗三藐三菩提心。譬如高原陆地不生莲华卑湿淤泥乃生此华。如是见无为法入正位者。终不复能生于佛法。烦恼泥中乃有众生起佛法耳。又如殖种于空终不得生。粪壤之地乃能滋茂。如是入无为正位者不生佛法。起于我见如须弥山。犹能发于阿耨多罗三藐三菩提心生佛法矣。是故当知一切烦恼为如来种。譬如不下巨海不能得无价宝珠。如是不入烦恼大海。则不能得一切智宝尔时大迦叶叹言。善哉善哉文殊师利。快说此语诚如所言。尘劳之畴为如来种。我等今者不复堪任发阿耨多罗三藐三菩提心。乃至五无间罪。犹能发意生于佛法。而今我等永不能发。譬如根败之士其于五欲不能复利。如是声闻诸结断者。于佛法中无所复益永不志愿。是故文殊师利。凡夫于佛法有返复。而声闻无也。所以者何。凡夫闻佛法能起无上道心不断三宝。正使声闻终身闻佛法力无畏等。永不能发无上道意尔时会中有菩萨名普现色身。问维摩诘言。居士。父母妻子亲戚眷属吏民知识悉为是谁。奴婢僮仆象马车乘皆何所在。于是维摩诘以偈答曰智度菩萨母法喜以为妻弟子众尘劳诸度法等侣总持之园苑八解之浴池方便以为父慈悲心为女随意之所转四摄为伎女无漏法林树定水湛然满一切众导师善心诚实男道品善知识歌咏诵法言觉意净妙华布以七净华无不由是生毕竟空寂舍由是成正觉以此为音乐解脱智慧果浴此无垢人象马五通驰相具以严容富有七财宝四禅为床座甘露法之食摧灭烦恼贼虽知无起灭供养于十方虽知诸佛国诸有众生类觉知众魔事或示老病死或现劫尽烧无数亿众生经书禁咒术世间众道法或作日月天劫中有疾疫劫中有饥馑劫中有刀兵若有大战阵一切国土中一切国土中示受于五欲火中生莲华或现作婬女或为邑中主诸有贫穷者我心憍慢者其有恐惧众或现离婬欲见须供事者随彼之所须如是道无量假令一切佛谁闻如是法大乘以为车众好饰其姿教授以滋息从于净命生解脱味为浆勇健无能逾示彼故有生无量亿如来及与众生空形声及威仪而示随其行成就诸群生天地皆洞然俱来请菩萨工巧诸伎艺悉于中出家梵王世界主现作诸药草现身作饮食为之起慈心立之以等力诸有地狱处畜生相食噉亦复现行禅是可谓希有引诸好色者或作商人导现作无尽藏为现大力士居前而慰安为五通仙人现为作僮仆得入于佛道所行无有涯于无量亿劫不发菩提心调御以一心惭愧之上服如所说修行多闻增智慧净心以澡浴降伏四种魔悉现诸国土诸佛及己身而常修净土无畏力菩萨以善方便智了知如幻化众人有常想一时到其舍尽现行此事因以解人惑或时作地水若有服之者先救彼饥渴化彼诸众生菩萨现威势辄往到于彼皆现生于彼令魔心愦乱在欲而行禅先以欲钩牵国师及大臣因以劝导之消伏诸贡高先施以无畏开导诸群生既悦可其意以善方便力智慧无边际赞叹其功德除彼不肖人游于八正路深心为华鬘回向为大利以为自觉音戒品为涂香胜幡建道场如日无不见无有分别想教化于群生一时能尽现随意皆能现通达无有碍照令知无常化令向佛道饶益诸群生而不堕邪见或复作风火除病消众毒等以法语人令住无诤地降伏使和安勉济其苦恼为之作利益不能得其便希有亦如是后令入佛道以祐利众生令发菩提心令住无上道后令发道心令住戒忍慈乃发以道心皆能给足之度脱无数众犹尚不能尽痴冥无智者

逐句解释

文殊问：何等为如来种——烦恼即菩提的根基

文殊师利问：什么是如来的种子？维摩诘答：一切烦恼，是如来种。这是全章最震撼的一句话：成佛的种子正是烦恼本身，而不是对烦恼的消除。就像莲花生于淤泥，如来身从烦恼中生；高原干地不生莲华，涅槃境界也不能生出菩提心来。这把修行的逻辑彻底颠倒：不是「先净化烦恼，后得解脱」，而是「从烦恼中生出解脱」。

Manjushri asked: what are the seeds of the Tathagata? Vimalakirti answered: all the defilements are the seeds of the Tathagata — just as the lotus grows in mud but not on dry high ground, bodhicitta is born from defilement but not from the pure grounds of nirvana

This is perhaps the sutra's most radical claim about the nature of the path. In many Buddhist presentations, defilements are the enemy — the obstacle to awakening that must be eradicated. Here, Vimalakirti reverses this entirely: the defilements are the very soil in which awakening grows. A being who has already attained the pure nirvana of the Hinayana path has left behind the very conditions (ignorance, craving, attachment) from which bodhicitta arises. The lotus cannot grow in the air — it needs mud. The path needs the raw material of human suffering and confusion.

行于非道，是为通达佛道——入邪路以显正道

维摩诘说：行于非道，是为通达佛道。行贪欲、嗔恚、愚痴，行于邪见，行五无间，行魔事——通过进入这些道路（以方便的眼光），菩萨得以真正了解众生的处境，从而更有效地引导他们走上解脱之路。这不是纵容烦恼，而是以无所执著的心深入烦恼，如同以毒攻毒。

Vimalakirti teaches: going through the path of non-path is how to master the Buddha-path — by entering desire, anger, delusion, wrong views, and even the paths of the five grave offences, the bodhisattva gains direct understanding of where beings are, and can guide them from within

This teaching must be understood in the context of the bodhisattva's motivations and realisation. It is not a licence for moral carelessness. The bodhisattva who enters 'the path of non-path' does so without attachment, without self-deception, and without being pulled along by the currents of craving that affect ordinary beings. The difference between the bodhisattva who visits the brothel (as in Chapter 2) and an ordinary person visiting the brothel is precisely this: the bodhisattva is not there for the ordinary reason, and is not affected by it in the ordinary way.

示受于贪欲，然不依染着——方便示行，心无执著

维摩诘进一步列举：示受于贪欲，而不失禅定；示有妻子，常修梵行；示有家属，乐求远离；示着五欲，不染世乐；示有疾病，而常精进；示为老态，而深修道。每一对都是外表与内心的反差——这正是「方便」的极致表达：外相随机因缘，内心不离解脱。

Vimalakirti lists the bodhisattva's paradoxical modes: apparently indulging in desire yet not losing meditative stillness; appearing to have a family yet always practising celibacy; appearing to enjoy the five pleasures yet not stained by worldly delight; appearing old yet deeply practising the path

These paradoxes — already introduced in Chapter 2 — reappear here in a richer context. The bodhisattva's whole life is a sustained performance of skillful means, where the external appearance is calibrated to reach specific beings, while the inner state is always undisturbed. This is not hypocrisy; it is a form of compassionate mime. The actor playing a villain in a drama is not a villain; the bodhisattva appearing to be attached is not attached. What makes this possible is the complete absence of a fixed self that could actually be sullied by the appearance.

维摩诘现八种不可思议——与文殊的深度对话

本章的中段是维摩诘与文殊的一系列深度对话，涉及：如何入各种三昧而不舍一切法；如何在众生中显现各种相（佛相、菩萨相、声闻相、乃至魔相）以度化不同根器；如何以邪正二道庄严佛土。这些对话展示了究竟位菩萨的境界：一切相都是方便，一切道都是佛道。

The middle section of this chapter is a rapid dialogue between Vimalakirti and Manjushri covering: how to enter all samadhis without abandoning any teaching; how to manifest as a Buddha, bodhisattva, disciple, or even a demon in order to reach beings of different capacities; how to adorn the Buddha-land through both right and wrong paths

The dialogue accelerates through a remarkable range of teachings. The consistent theme is that for the fully realised bodhisattva, the distinction between 'right' and 'wrong' paths is a conventional tool rather than an absolute boundary. Everything — including the appearance of wrongdoing — can be used as a vehicle for liberation. The risk of misunderstanding this teaching is obvious, and the sutra implicitly guards against it by tying these capacities to the complete realisation of emptiness and compassion.

一切烦恼为佛种——高原无莲，泥中有华

维摩诘以莲华比喻总结整章：高原陆地不生莲华，卑湿淤泥乃生此华。如是见无为法入正位者，终不能复发无上菩提心。烦恼泥中乃有众生起佛法矣。这是对整章「非道即道」主题的最后一次概括：菩萨之所以能度化众生，正是因为没有逃离众生所在的泥土。

Vimalakirti uses the lotus as a final summation: lotuses do not grow on dry high ground — they grow in muddy wetlands; likewise, those who have already entered the pure nirvana of no-arising cannot generate bodhicitta. It is from within the mud of defilement that the lotus of awakening grows

The lotus image appears throughout Buddhist literature, but Vimalakirti uses it here with a specific and provocative twist. The usual reading is: the lotus grows in mud but is unstained by it — meaning the practitioner lives in the world but is not affected by it. Vimalakirti's reading goes further: the mud is not just what the lotus must overcome; it is the necessary condition for the lotus to exist at all. Without the mud of ignorance and craving, there would be no material for awakening to work with. This is why the bodhisattva refuses the premature peace of nirvana and remains in the midst of suffering beings.

普现色身三昧——一身现于一切身

文殊问：何谓菩萨行于非道？维摩诘以「普现色身三昧」作结：此三昧中，菩萨能普入一切众生身，随所应化而现其身。医王入病室而不染病；智者入痴迷而不迷惑。这是整章「非道即道」的终极实践：菩萨能完全进入任何一种存在形态，同时不失其本来清净的自性。

Vimalakirti concludes with the 'samadhi of universally manifesting form-bodies': in this samadhi the bodhisattva can enter any being's body and appear in any form needed for liberation — like a physician entering a sick ward without contracting illness, or a wise person entering confusion without becoming confused

The 'samadhi of universally manifesting form-bodies' is the practical expression of everything this chapter has been teaching. The bodhisattva's ability to appear in any form — including wrathful, deluded, or demonic forms — without being those things is grounded in the realisation of emptiness. Because there is no fixed self, there is nothing that can be permanently stained or altered by any appearance. The physician analogy is the most accessible: the doctor who treats illness does not become ill; the bodhisattva who enters delusion does not become deluded. The protection is not distance but realisation.

总结 · Summary

佛道品以「一切烦恼是如来种」开篇，颠覆了「先净化后解脱」的修行逻辑，将烦恼重新定位为菩提的生长土壤。贯穿全章的核心命题是「行于非道，是为通达佛道」：菩萨通过方便进入各种世间道路（包括贪欲、邪见，乃至魔道），以直接理解众生所在处境，从而能够在最深处引导众生。这一切的可能条件，是菩萨彻底了达无我与空性，使任何外相的示现都不会污染其本性。章末以莲花为喻：菩萨不离泥土，正因如此，才能开出解脱的莲华。

Chapter 8 is the sutra's most challenging teaching on the nature of the path. It opens with the claim that defilements are the seeds of Buddhahood — turning the standard purification narrative on its head. The bodhisattva's path is not away from the world's mud but deeper into it, armed with the unshakeable recognition that mud has no ultimate solidity. The chapter's central proposition — 'going through the path of non-path is how to master the Buddha-path' — describes the bodhisattva's paradoxical engagement with every form of experience, including those normally considered spiritually dangerous. The lotus image at the chapter's end gives this a concrete form: awakening grows precisely in the conditions — ignorance, craving, the whole range of human defilement — that the path is meant to address. Remove the mud and you remove the lotus.
